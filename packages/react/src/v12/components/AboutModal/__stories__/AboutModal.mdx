# About modal

A small passive modal that says what a product is and which version runs. It
shows a logo over the title, the content, a row of links, additional information
and a copyright line. One additional information item shows as a block; several
show as tabs. It follows `open` and closes itself unless `onClose` returns
`false`. The close button, Escape or a press outside closes it, and focus goes
back to the launcher.

## Motion

- **The modal's snap:** the overlay fades in, and the surface arrives centred
  from 92% of its box, leaning toward what opened it. Leaving, it pulls back.
  `backdrop` and `shadow` are the modal's.
- **Content:** the body moves in with the frame, as in the modal.
- **Tabs:** with several additional information items, the marker glides between
  tabs, and the panels swap out, then in. `timeScale` reaches the tabs too.

## Reduced motion

The modal's: the overlay and the surface only fade, at their final geometry, on
half the clock. The tabs' selector fades on the new tab on half the clock, and
the panels swap at once.
