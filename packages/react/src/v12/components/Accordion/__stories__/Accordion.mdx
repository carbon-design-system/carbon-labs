# Accordion

Headings that open and shut the content under them. `Accordion` holds the items.
`AccordionItem` is a heading button with a chevron, over the body it opens.
`AccordionSkeleton` is the accordion while it loads. They take Carbon's props,
plus `exclusive`, `headingLevel`, `corners`, `frame` and `timeScale`. An item's
`open` is its initial state, and it is followed whenever it changes; a click
toggles it in between. Enter or Space toggles an item; ↑, ↓, Home and End move
between the headings.

## Motion

The body is the shared disclosure.

- **Body:** the height travels on the step for its travel: under 24px fast-02,
  under 120px moderate-01, under 480px moderate-02, under 900px moderate-03,
  else slow-01. Opening is on entrance-productive. Shutting is one step faster,
  on exit-productive.
- **One motion:** every body that changes in one commit moves on one clock, the
  step of the farthest travel. Expand all and collapse all are one motion. With
  more than one body, the step is never slower than moderate-02, before a shut's
  step down. With `exclusive`, opening one item shuts the other, as one swap on
  standard-productive.
- **Soft edge:** while it moves, the clip's bottom fades over
  `min(16px, h, H − h)`, so it is gone on the frame the body lands.
- **Content in:** the content waits 8px above and comes down into place from the
  opening's first frame, over one duration token longer than the body. It
  settles last.
- **Content out:** the content holds still while the edge closes over it.
- **Turn:** a change mid-move starts from the body's live height and takes the
  step of what is left.
- **Chevron:** down when shut, up when open. The heading is its motion icon
  frame.
- **Rollover:** the kit's rollover on the heading, an even wash across the row.
  It is flush: the row never grows past its hairlines or shrinks from them.
- **First paint:** no motion. Under `exclusive`, the last open item stays open.

## Reduced motion

A body opens or shuts at once, with no soft edge. The content fades in on the
reduced clock. The chevron and the hover change at once.
