# ActivityFeed

Events in time order, newest first, grouped by day. Each day has a sticky header
that never folds. An event is an avatar with initials, who did what, a status,
its time and an optional detail behind Show details. An event that asks for an
answer can have up to two actions, drawn as small ghost buttons. Today's times
are relative; an earlier day shows the clock time. `categories` adds filter tags
over the list. While `loading`, skeleton rows stand in for the events. With
none, the feed shows an empty state. Give the feed a height: it scrolls inside
it.

## Motion

- **List arrival:** with the reader at the top, a new event's place opens from
  nothing on the step for its height, on entrance-productive. What is below
  follows. Its content comes down 8px into the place. A new day's place opens
  with its header.
- **Place hold:** with the reader further down, nothing in view moves. The row
  being read holds its place as events arrive above it, and Jump to latest shows
  with a count of the new events.
- **Floating snap:** Jump to latest is the kit's JumpTo, set to the top. The
  pill arrives and leaves on its own clock. A jump glides to the top on a clock
  set by the distance.
- **Disclosure:** Show details opens an event's detail with the kit's
  disclosure. The body opens on the step for its travel, and the content moves
  with the opening.
- **Content swap:** a filter change swaps the list out, then in. The events and
  day titles leave 8px up on fast-01, exit-productive. The new ones come in from
  8px below on fast-02, entrance-productive. The top day title holds still when
  its day leads both lists. The view goes back to the top.
- **Soft edge:** under a stuck day title, a divider and a soft edge grow over
  the first 16px of scroll under it, so neither pops.
- **Skeleton reveal:** when the events land, the skeleton dissolves in place as
  they fade in.
- **Stillness:** as a minute passes, a time's words change in place. Only the
  times whose words change render again, never a row or the feed.

## Reduced motion

A new event's place is there at once, and its content fades in on the reduced
clock. A filter change swaps the list at once. A detail opens at once, its
content fading in. Jump to latest lands at once, with no glide.
