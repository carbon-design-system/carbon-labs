# App shell

The frame an app sits in: a header, a nav, the page, and an optional side panel.
`AppShell` takes the header, the nav (a SideNav) and a `panel`; the page is its
children.

The shell sizes itself from its own width, not the window's:

| Size class | Width          | Nav                           | Panel            |
| ---------- | -------------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Compact    | under 672px    | overlay, from the menu button | full-width sheet |
| Medium     | 672 to 1055px  | rail of icons                 | covers the page  |
| Expanded   | 1056 to 1311px | column                        | pushes the page  |
| Large      | 1312px and up  | column                        | pushes the page  |

- `scope="page"` fixes the header and scrolls the page with the window;
  `scope="container"` fills its container.
- `frame="inset"` puts the page on a rounded workspace, and
  `headerPlacement="nav"` puts the header at the top of the nav column.
- The header and nav are ghost by default, on chrome that is dark in every theme
  (`chromeTheme`). The page follows the theme; `mainTheme` gives it one of its
  own, and `inverse` is the opposite of the theme's.
- `Header` is the 48px global header, `NavOverlay` the overlay nav alone, and
  `Region` one area of a screen, with its surface, corners and scroll.

## Motion

Most of the motion is the parts' own: the rail's fold is the SideNav's, and the
overlay nav and the panel move as side panels.

- **Fold:** with `foldTo="hide"`, the column's width moves to a 56px strip and
  back in one motion, and main follows its edge. The content holds its open
  width while the edge passes over it, and the strip swaps in when it lands. The
  clock is the step for the width's travel, on entrance-productive; shrinking,
  one step faster, on exit-productive. Pressed mid-fold, it turns round from the
  width drawn. `foldTo="overlay"` folds the column away and opens the nav from
  the menu button.
- **Overlay nav:** at compact, the nav is a SidePanel at the start, under the
  header. It snaps in timed by its width, leaves a step shorter, and its content
  rides the opening. The dim fades on the side panel's overlay clocks.
- **Panel push:** at expanded and large, the panel is SidePanel's `slideIn`:
  main makes room on the panel's clock.
- **Class edge:** crossing a size class edge while the panel is open re-places
  it at once. It is the same panel, with no second entrance, its content and
  focus kept. Main's room is given or taken at once.
- **Menu button:** while the overlay is open, the header's menu button is its
  Close. The Menu icon match-cuts to Close, and the button's name changes with
  it.
- **Home link:** the product name has the family's rollover, a glow inset 4px
  from the bar.

## Reduced motion

The hide fold moves at once. The overlay nav and its dim fade on the reduced
clock. Main makes room for the panel at once. The menu icon changes at once. A
class edge is at once either way.
