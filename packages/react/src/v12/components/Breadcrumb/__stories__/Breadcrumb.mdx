# Breadcrumb

A trail of links to the levels above the current page. A `nav` holds an ordered
list of `BreadcrumbItem`s. Each one is a link, plain text, an app's own link
element, or an `OverflowMenu` for collapsed levels. The current page is primary
text, marked `aria-current`. In a narrow container the crumbs wrap; each crumb
stays on one line.

## Motion

- **Rollover:** each link crumb takes the link rollover: a glow in the letters,
  the press and the release circle. The underline fades in on hover. The current
  page and plain text take no rollover.
- **Overflow menu:** a crumb holding an `OverflowMenu` shows the horizontal
  overflow icon at the breadcrumb's size. Its menu opens on the Menu's snap,
  floating: md, or sm in an sm breadcrumb.
- **Trail:** a change to the trail reflows as one change. A crumb that leaves
  fades out first, over fast-01. Then the crumbs that stay slide to their new
  places, timed by travel. Then a new crumb snaps in, with no fade, sliding from
  the trail's side. Keyed children reflow by key.
- **Removal:** a trail that only loses crumbs plays together: the crumb fades
  out as the others close the gap.
- **Slash:** with `noTrailingSlash`, the last crumb's slash fades out and keeps
  its place, so nothing jumps. A crumb that stops being last fades its slash in.
- **Interruption:** a new change mid-reflow turns round from where the crumbs
  are on screen.
- **Slow motion:** `timeScale` multiplies the reflow's durations. An overflow
  menu takes it too.

## Reduced motion

Nothing slides. A crumb that leaves fades out on the reduced clock, then the new
places and the new crumbs appear at once. A slash fades in on the reduced clock.
The overflow menu opens and closes at once.
