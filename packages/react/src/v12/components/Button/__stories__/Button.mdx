# Button

An action's trigger, with Carbon's Button props. `kind` is the role: primary,
secondary or danger. `variant` is the treatment: filled, outlined or tonal.
`frame` is what it stands on: inset, ghost, or none for a text button; it wins
over `variant`. Buttons are pills by default. `busy` shows a spinner in the
icon's place while an action runs. The package also exports `ButtonSet`,
`ButtonSkeleton` and `ButtonGrid`, a set of equal actions in one shell with one
Tab stop.

## Motion

- **Hover:** a glow of the matched-lightness hover color under the pointer, in
  on fast-02 and out on fast-01.
- **Size:** the button grows 1px on hover and presses 1px in from its rest edge.
- **Press and release:** the flat pressed color while held. On release, the
  pressed color recedes from the release point as a soft circle. A press dragged
  off the button is cancelled; dragged back on, still held, it presses again.
- **Toned steps:** outlined and ghost buttons hover and press in tints of their
  own color. The label takes one Carbon step further from the backdrop, and more
  if it would fall under 4.5:1.
- **Icon:** a motion icon plays on the button's hover or keyboard focus.
- **Busy:** out, then in. The icon fades out, then the spinner draws on
  (moderate-01). When busy ends, the spinner closes (fast-02), then the icon
  fades back. With no icon, the label takes the icon's turn. An icon with its
  own pending motion, such as Save or Send, keeps it, with no spinner.
- **Focus:** keyboard only. A 2px ring in the button's own text color just
  inside its edge, with a 2px rim of the fill round it on a filled button.
- **ButtonGrid:** each cell has the Button's hover, press and release circle,
  clipped to the cell, with no growth or press-in.
- **ButtonSkeleton:** it pulses on the page's skeleton wave.

## Reduced motion

The glow, the 1px growth and the press still show, at once. The release circle
and a toned label's step land at once. A motion icon is a still drawing and does
not play. Busy swaps at once, and the spinner is its still dots.
