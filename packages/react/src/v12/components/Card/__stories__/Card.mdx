# Card

A tile with a header, a body, actions and a footer, on `--cds-surface` with 8px
corners and 16px in. A card has a label, a title and a description, a body,
action icons and an overflow menu, and primary and secondary buttons. With
`onClick` it is a control, one tab stop; without it, it is a plain surface.
`ProductiveCard` is dense, for product UI, with its actions at the top and a
ghost primary button. Its `clickZone` sets where a click counts.
`ExpressiveCard` holds media to the card's edge, or a pictogram, with its
actions at the bottom. `CardSkeleton` is a card while it loads. `decorator` and
`slug` are accepted and render nothing.

## Motion

- **Rollover, text card:** a clickable card takes the tiles' quiet rollover,
  painted behind the content, never over it. The surface grows 1px on hover and
  presses 1px in. `hoverSteps="standard"` uses the house steps.
- **Rollover, card with controls:** a clickable card that holds controls hovers
  with a ring.
- **Controls inside:** when a control in the card is hovered or pressed, the
  card yields and its cast shadow steps back. The controls never take the card's
  colors, and a click on one never clicks the card. Outside the click zone, a
  click does nothing and the hover goes back to rest.
- **Cast shadow:** off by default; `shadow` turns it on for a clickable card. It
  is lit from the pointer: the offset follows at once, and the strength fades on
  moderate-01. A press draws it in.
- **Snap:** the overflow menu opens and closes with the kit's menu.
- **Motion icons:** the action icons, the overflow trigger and the button icons
  are motion icons, played by their own buttons.
- `timeScale` multiplies the durations of the rollover, the buttons and the
  overflow menu.

## Reduced motion

The rollover changes at once. The cast shadow's strength changes at once.
