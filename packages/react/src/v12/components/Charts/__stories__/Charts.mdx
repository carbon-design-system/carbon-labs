# Charts

The base the v12 chart family is built on. It holds what every chart shares
and nothing that draws a plot: the data types, the tuning values
(`CHART_DESIGN`), the motion resolved per motion level, the color roles and
the series palette, the line constants, the series key, and the card's sizes.
The root entry loads no chart runtime, so a card without a plot stays small.
The plot's shared pieces come at `@carbon-labs/react-charts/plot`.

## The chart card

`ChartCard` is the card every chart sits in, on the lane's Card: a title and
its overflow menu, the metric and its delta, the plot, a headline and
description, a status line, and up to two buttons, the first primary.

<Canvas of={ChartsStories.Large} />

- **Metric:** the number, its fraction and its unit. The delta takes its
  `kind`'s color: `success`, `danger` or `default`. A `warning` delta keeps
  the text's color and shows the warning mark, since that color is too light
  for text.
- **Theme:** the page's, or `light`, `dark` or `inverse` of its own.
- **States:** `loading` shows the lane's skeleton; `empty` and `error` show a
  message where the plot goes.
- **Accessibility:** a group named by its title. The metric and delta read as
  one line, "12.4K requests, up 8%". The overflow menu has a name.
- **Right to left:** the header, metric and foot mirror; the plot stays left
  to right.
- **Motion:** a new value rolls only the characters that change. Under reduced
  motion it lands at once.

## The family

Each family is its own package on this base:

- **BarChart:** single, grouped, stacked, range and horizontal bars.
- **RadialChart:** a ring against a limit, and rings that expand.
- **AreaChart:** a time series, with a range selector.
- **DotMatrix:** one value per row and column.
- **ConeChart:** small multiples, each projected to a range.
- **StatusCard:** a reading, how fresh it is, and where it is heading.

## Color

A chart's frame (text, lines, surfaces, focus, status) reads the v12 theme's
tokens. Text is `text-primary` or `text-secondary`, the two that hold 4.5:1 on
the page and the card; axis labels are `text-secondary`. The data colors are
the palette: Carbon's color steps for series, and the v12 neutral for measured
series. They are stored in OKLCH and drawn in sRGB.

- **Large marks** (bars, arcs, areas) hold 3:1 against the page and the card.
- **Small marks** (texture dots, lines of 3px or less) hold 4.5:1; a step that
  falls short moves away from the card until it holds.
- A chart that shows status leaves magenta out.

## Data

A series is measured up to `now` and projected after it. A projected value can
carry a range (`low`, `high`). Values and axis labels format through `Intl` in
the chart's `locale`.

## Sizes

- `sm`: 380px.
- `md`: 405px, the summary card, its plot to the card's edges.
- `lg`: 640px.

## Reduced motion

At the reduced level every change fades in place on the reduced clock: no
front, no stagger, no rise. At `none` every change lands at once.

## Demo data

The stories use neutral demo data for every chart, at `sm` and `lg`, and one
reading later for live updates. It is not part of the package's entry.
