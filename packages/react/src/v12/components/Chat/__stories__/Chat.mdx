# Chat shell

A chat with an assistant: the conversation, the prompt below it, and what docks
round the prompt. Use it as a page of its own, or in a pane beside a page.

- `ChatShell` holds the header, the conversation and the prompt. It sets the
  look of the parts inside (`variant`, `corners`) and caps them at a reading
  width (`column`).
- `ChatTranscript` is the conversation: the turns, and for each reply the line
  that says what it's doing and the steps it took.
- `PromptInput` is the prompt: a field that grows, its footer controls, Stop,
  attachments and a notice.
- `ChatDock` puts a follow-up question (`FollowUpCard`) above the prompt and
  suggestions (`Suggestions`) below it.
- `ChatTurnNavigator` lists the questions in a long chat and scrolls to the one
  you choose.
- `ChatPane` opens the chat beside a page.
- `ChatMessage` and `FileChips` are the smaller parts, for building your own.

A reply's status row and its steps, `TurnStatus` and `StepList`, have their own
pages.

## Working together

- **One thing works at a time.** While a reply is coming, only one part shows
  it: the status line, a running step, the streaming words, or a ring on Stop
  when the reply is out of sight. Each part does this only when asked
  (TurnStatus `working`, StepList `workingStep`, PromptInput `stopRing`);
  ChatTranscript sets the first two and reports `onReplyInView`.
- **The question list.** In the header, ChatTurnNavigator reads the shell's
  conversation. Each question shows its first line and its time, and the marker
  is on the one in view. Alt with ↑ or ↓ steps between questions while focus is
  in the conversation, not on a control.
- **Dictation.** The microphone is a toggle. A press asks to listen
  (`onListeningChange`). While the host waits for the microphone, it passes
  `connecting` and a spinner takes the icon's place; the button is pressed and
  named "Stop dictation", but not filled. Once it listens, it passes
  `listening`; the button fills and the pills follow `level`, a number from 0 to
  1 or a function read each frame. A press while connecting or listening stops.
  A polite region says "Listening" and "Dictation stopped".
- **Custom parts.** `useChatVariant` gives a part of your own the shell's
  `variant`.

## Motion

- **Content swap in a layout move:** in ChatDock's slots, what leaves plays the
  swap's out toward the prompt (fast-02, exit-productive, 16px). Then every
  changing slot resizes on one clock by the larger travel, standard-productive.
  Then what arrives plays the swap's in (moderate-01, entrance-productive).
- **Arrival:** new turns slide the column up by what they push it, by travel,
  standard-productive. A person's turn fades up in place (fast-02).
- **Pinned scroll:** a reader at the bottom stays there; one who scrolled up is
  never moved.
- **Streaming:** a reply's first words rise 4px as they land (fast-02,
  entrance-productive); then each sentence fades up in place.
- **History:** replies done as the conversation mounts are there, still: no
  stream and no wait for the summary.
- **Send to Stop:** the arrow swaps out, then the square in. The Spinner turns
  round it only while the reply is out of sight.
- **Width move:** ChatPane's width moves by travel on standard-expressive, one
  step faster shutting, its content under the 16px soft edge.
- **Sideways opening:** a file chip opens its space by travel,
  entrance-productive, its content moving with the opening under a soft edge.
- **Dictation:** connecting, the button stays ghost; the microphone fades out
  (fast-01) and the Spinner draws on. Listening, the fill fades in (fast-01);
  the microphone leaves (fast-02), then three dots open into pills (moderate-02)
  that rise with the level on fast-01 and fall on moderate-01. Stopping plays
  back a step faster, and the fill fades once the pills have folded.
- **Rise:** the docked notice rises from behind the field by travel. Its content
  swaps in place; it sinks one step faster.
- **Jump to a question:** the navigator's popover snaps from its button. A
  choice closes it, then the conversation glides to the question and focuses it;
  a wash fades in (fast-02) and out (slow-01).
- **Marker:** in the navigator's list, on the turn in view, moving in place as
  the reader scrolls; never a glide.
- **Selection:** in Suggestions and FollowUpCard, the selector grows in from the
  row's center (fast-02), then the choice is handed on.

## Reduced motion

No travel: fades on the reduced clock, moves landing at once. The outs are at
once, so the working element passes at once, and the first words have no rise. A
jump to a question has no wash. Dictation's swap, its icon change and its fill
land at once, and the pills hold still. Popovers and panes follow their own
reduced rules.
