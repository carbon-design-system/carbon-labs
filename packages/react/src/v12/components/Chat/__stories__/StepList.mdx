# Step list

The steps the machine takes, as they happen: one row per step with its status
mark, its words and its time, joined by a rule. Use it for a reply's steps, an
agent's workflow or a job's stages. A step is queued, running, waiting, done,
failed or cancelled; a tool call with `input` or `output` opens to show them.
`collapsible` adds a counter line that opens and shuts the rows.

Screen readers hear an ordered list named by `label`, each mark named by its
status, and one polite announcement per settled step. Every word is its own
prop.

## Motion

- **Status hand-off:** running to done, failed or waiting changes the mark in
  place: the spinner shrinks 4px, the disc grows from under it and the glyph
  draws on, with no fades.
- **Wind-up:** queued to running, the dashed circle shrinks to the spinner's
  size while turning, exit-productive; the spinner takes its place on that
  frame.
- **Words:** out, then in through the shimmer's band. The trailing words' place
  glides to its width by travel.
- **Arrival:** a new step's space opens by travel, its content coming from 8px
  back; the rule above grows down with it.
- **Stillness:** a change inside the last one's swap lands at once at its latest
  state; several at once land together.
- **Collapse:** the counter line opens and shuts the rows by the disclosure; its
  count changes at once and its chevron glides by travel. A finished list shuts
  one loading period after its last step ends; a failure or a wait opens it.
- **One working element:** with `workingStep`, only that running step shows its
  spinner and shimmer; the others keep the dashed circle, still.

## Reduced motion

Rows and marks land at once and the labels are plain; the words swap on the
reduced clock.
