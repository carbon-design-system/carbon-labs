# Turn status

A live status row for work the machine does: the working mark, the stage's words
and the elapsed seconds while it works, then a one-line summary that opens its
details. Use it above a reply, a job, a long action or an agent's run. The
stages (`status`, `label`) are the host's real ones and the time is real time
(`startedAt`, `endedAt`), with no percentage.

Screen readers hear one polite announcement per settled stage, never the
seconds; the mark is hidden from them. `useTurnRelease(startedAt)` holds a
host's words until the row has collapsed.

## Motion

- **Arrival:** the mark loads in at once and the row comes in through the
  shimmer's band, not a fade. A row first seen settled is simply there.
- **Stage words:** after one loading period, and on each change: out, then in
  through the band. Each label holds at least one period; a burst lands on the
  latest.
- **Seconds:** after three periods, in through the band. They change at once,
  with no counting, and glide by travel when the words before them change width.
- **Mark:** thinking pulses, acting turns a triangle, waiting a square. Acting
  to thinking wiggles once.
- **Summary:** out, then in. The mark shrinks out, the words leave and the
  details shut; then the summary comes in and `onCollapse` releases the host's
  words.
- **Error:** the mark shrinks out, then the error mark arrives by the status
  hand-off, with the cause and Retry.
- **Details:** the disclosure's: open while it works, shut as part of the out,
  opened from the summary.
- **One working element:** with `working` false, the mark shrinks out in its
  slot and the words still.

## Reduced motion

The mark is still and the words plain. The swaps run on the reduced clock; the
mark and the details change at once.
