## Overview

{/* carbon-labs-create:problem-statement */}

> **Problem statement:** A conversation needs its working material beside it
> (what it knows, the code it wrote, the files it was given, its settings and a
> canvas to arrange results on) without leaving the conversation to reach any of
> it.

`ChatPanels` holds which side panel is open. `ChatPanelToggles` is the row of
icon-only toggles in the surface's header, each naming its panel in a tooltip
and saying `aria-expanded`. `ChatPanelArea` is the content with the panel
opening along its inline end: pushed in a standalone chat, over the card when
embedded, and over the content below `md`.

Icon-only is a proposal: panel toggles are not on Carbon's icon-only allow-list
(`iconography.md` › Proposed).
