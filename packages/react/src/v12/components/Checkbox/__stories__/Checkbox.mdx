# Checkbox

A box that turns one option on or off. A parent of partly checked children shows
mixed, a dash. `Checkbox` takes Carbon's props, plus `disabledReason` and
`timeScale`. `CheckboxGroup` sets several checkboxes under one legend, stacked
or in a row. `CheckboxSkeleton` is the checkbox while it loads.

## Motion

- **Check:** the fill changes at once. 40ms later the check draws its stroke on,
  on fast-02 with standard-productive. The state reads first.
- **Uncheck:** the check fades out on fast-01.
- **Mixed:** the dash grows from its middle, on fast-02, 40ms after the fill. It
  is drawn from the input's own mixed state, so a click that clears it shows at
  once. Assistive tech hears "mixed".
- **Hover:** the rollover on the box. Off, a wash of the text color at 8%; on, a
  toned step of the fill. The glow fades in on fast-02 and out on fast-01. The
  box grows 1px. Hovering the label plays it too, however the label wraps.
- **Press:** no press color of its own. The box presses 1px in.
- **Status ring:** `invalid` and `warn` color the box's stroke and add 1px
  outside it, red or yellow. The rollover paints the ring, so it grows with the
  hover. Disabled and read-only show no status.
- **Message line:** helper, warning and error share one line and swap in place,
  out then in.
- **Focus:** keyboard only, 2px `$text-primary`, 2px outside the box; 3px out
  while a status shows. It has no transition.

## Reduced motion

The check and the dash appear at once. The hover changes at once. The press
still shows; the release and the return to hover are at once. The message line
changes at once.
