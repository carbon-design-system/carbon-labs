# ContentSwitcher

Pick one of two or more options or views. The switches sit in one track, and a
selector glides between them. `ContentSwitcher` takes Carbon's API:
`selectedIndex`, `activation`, `size`, `lowContrast`, `onChange` and disabled
switches. `Switch` is a labelled switch, with an optional icon before the label.
`IconSwitch` is an icon only, named by its tooltip. A switch's `aria-controls`
names the panel it shows; the selection kit's `SelectionPanels` swaps that
content.

## Motion

- **Glide:** a new selection sends the selector from where it is to the new
  switch, on standard-productive. Its duration comes from the distance, from
  fast-00 to moderate-01. Its leading edge runs ahead, then both edges land
  together. Labels change color, and weight, where it covers them. A new
  selection mid-glide starts from where the selector is.
- **Press and reach:** the pressed switch takes a flat pressed color, inset 1px,
  while held. A press on another switch starts the selector toward it before the
  release. Slide to another switch to retarget; slide off to cancel.
- **Drag:** on by default. The selector follows the pointer and lands on the
  nearest switch, stopping at the walls with a 4px squash.
- **Hover:** unselected switches take the rollover glow and a label color step.
  The selector glows a step off its own fill under the pointer. A switch just
  picked keeps its hover until the selector lands on it. A motion icon plays on
  its switch's hover.
- **Tooltip:** on icon switches, the first tooltip snaps in from the pointer.
  While it shows, it jumps from switch to switch. The pointer can move onto it.
- **Content swap:** `SelectionPanels` swaps out, then in, never overlapping. The
  old content moves 8px the way the selector goes and fades, on fast-01 and
  exit-productive. The new content follows from 8px behind, on fast-02 and
  entrance-productive. In right to left it still follows the selector on screen.
- **Container resize:** with `fit="content"`, the default, the panels' container
  resizes to the new panel as a window, starting with the in. `fit="largest"`
  holds the largest panel's size.
- **Loading:** `loading` puts placeholder switches in the switches' own boxes,
  each label a bar. The switches take them in order as one reflow along x.
  `skeletonCount` defaults to the switches on screen, else 3.
- **Keyboard:** ← and → wrap and skip disabled switches; Home and End go to the
  ends. In right to left, ← goes to the next switch and → to the previous. With
  `activation="automatic"` they select; with `manual` they move focus, and Enter
  or Space selects. A disabled switch with a `disabledReason` stays in the
  arrows' path and shows why in a tooltip.

## Reduced motion

No travel: the selector fades in on the new switch as it fades out of the old,
on half the clock. There is no reach. The pressed color still shows; the hover's
release is at once. Tooltips show and leave at once, with no drift. The new
panel replaces the old on one frame, and the container resizes at once. A
loading track changes width at once.
