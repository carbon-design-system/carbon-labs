# Contextual bar

A bar that appears while something is true, such as a selection or unsaved
changes, and goes when it isn't. It follows `open`, and `label` names its
region. `placement="inline"`, the default, sits in the flow and opens room for
itself, so the content below glides. `overlay` floats over the content as a pill
and never takes room. `toolbar` lies over the first line of its positioned
container, in a toolbar row's place. It isn't sticky, takes no room, and hides
the row's other children while it is there. Inline and overlay bars are sticky,
8px below `--cds-contextual-bar-top`, 3rem by default. `variant="tonal"`, the
default on the page, is a fill one lightness step off its backdrop, with a
hairline. `inverse`, the default in an inset workspace, takes the page's
opposite theme. `kind` is a status. On tonal it colors the outline, solved to
3:1 against the fill. On inverse it fills the bar, stepped in lightness until
the text holds 4.5:1. A tonal bar is info unless `kind` says otherwise, and
`null` gives none. What's in it steps up one layer. Filled and outlined controls
in it step their corners down from the bar's, and ghost controls hover full
round. `ContextualBar.Count` is a number that rolls, and `ContextualBar.Swap`
swaps its content out, then in, by `swapKey`.

## Motion

- **One ease:** the bar's edges, its room and the mask round it move on one
  clock and one curve, standard-productive, and land on the same frame. The
  clock is the step for the farthest travel, its side edges' or its height (its
  room's, inline). Out is one step shorter. The bar doesn't fade: it travels and
  clips.
- **Snap in:** it grows from its long edge nearer the press. That edge holds
  still and the other travels away from it. It widens from 75% about the point
  across from the press, no further from the middle than `originBand` of its
  width. From a key, the key's target stands in for the press; with neither, the
  middle of its top edge. Inline and at rest, its room pushes the rows down, so
  it grows from its top edge. In a toolbar row, it grows from its bottom edge,
  at the end on the side of the press.
- **Fold out:** the far edge pulls back into the edge it grew from, with the
  width held. It shows what it last showed, not the change that closed it.
- **Content:** the row starts 8px back, against the way the bar grows, and moves
  into place over just past the bar's clock, on entrance-productive. The soft
  edge at the moving edge, `min(16px, h, H − h)`, reveals it. Out, it holds
  still while the soft edge closes over it. Reopened as it folds, it goes on
  from where it stopped.
- **Sliver guard:** the surface's opacity is `min(1, height / 4px)` in every
  placement, so it is opaque on every frame from 4px tall.
- **Content fade:** its contents wait, unseen, until the bar's start edge has
  passed them, so nothing is drawn cut. Over content, or when they wait, they
  fade up over 40ms on a clock of 220ms or less, else 80ms. Over content they
  fade out over the end of the fold.
- **Room:** inline, its room opens and closes on the bar's clock and curve in
  one layout transaction, so the content below glides. A change in its height,
  its content wrapping onto another line or back, glides too. In a container out
  of the flow it opens no room.
- **Dock mask:** carried over content, what's around it fades out like a soft
  edge: nothing shows above the bar, and the soft edge runs below it. The mask's
  strength moves on the bar's clock, and the mask follows the moving edge.
- **Count:** `ContextualBar.Count` rolls up as the number rises and down as it
  falls. Its label rolls with it.
- **Swap:** a new `swapKey` swaps the content out, then in, never overlapping.
  Its width glides from the old content's to the new, so what follows glides
  too. `axis` and `direction` set where the new content comes from.
- **Stillness:** under a still pointer, nothing it comes over or uncovers lights
  its hover until the pointer moves.
- `timeScale` slows the snap, the room, the count and the swap together.

## Reduced motion

The bar, its room and its content are there or gone at once, and the mask with
them. Inline, the scroll takes up the room, so the content below holds still on
screen. Counts change in place, and swaps snap.
