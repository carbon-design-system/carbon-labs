# CopyButton

Carbon's CopyButton, prop for prop, on the kit's IconButton. Its description is
the tooltip and the accessible name. A click turns the tooltip's text into the
feedback for `feedbackTimeout`, and another click restarts it. It does not write
the clipboard: `onClick` does. The feedback is also read out once per copy from
a polite live region. The frame is ghost by default; `frame="inset"` gives the
button its own surface. `useCopyFeedback` gives the same feedback to other
triggers.

## Motion

- **Rollover:** IconButton's. A glow under the pointer, the 1px growth on hover
  and the press, the release circle, and a keyboard ring in the icon's color.
- **Copied mark:** a copy closes the two pages into one square, which cuts to a
  checkmark, on moderate-02. It goes back to Copy as the feedback ends, on
  moderate-01.
- **Icon hover:** the hover of whichever icon shows plays on the button's hover
  or focus.
- **Tooltip:** it arrives and leaves on the snap.
- **Feedback roll:** the description moves out to the side and the feedback
  comes in from the other, whole, on moderate-01. The tooltip resizes round the
  text as it moves. After `feedbackTimeout` it goes back the other way. If the
  pointer and focus have just left, the text waits for the tooltip to be gone,
  so a leaving tooltip never changes its text.
- **Re-placing:** the tooltip is placed again on every frame its box resizes, so
  it stays centered on the button.
- **Slow motion:** `TooltipMotion`'s `timeScale` reaches the tooltip, the
  rollover and the roll.

## Reduced motion

The icon swaps to the checkmark and back at once, and it has no hover. The text
swaps at once, with no roll. The rollover's glow, growth and release land at
once.
