# DataTable

Rows of resources in columns, with Carbon's DataTable API: its render props, its
parts and its props. `DataTable` adds `corners`: `square`, the default, or
`rounded`, 8px on the outer box. `Table` sits in the kit's Scroller, so a table
wider than its container scrolls in place. Before it scrolls, cells kept on one
line may wrap so the table fits. `TableSelectRow` and `TableSelectAll` use the
kit's Checkbox. Select-all is checked when every selectable row in view is
selected, and mixed when some are. A sortable `TableHeader` draws one motion
icon for its sort. `TableExpandRow` and `TableExpandedRow` open a row's details.
The toolbar, its search and the batch actions bar are Carbon's.
`DataTableSkeleton` is the table while it loads. `DataTableReveal` is a table
whose body loads.

## Motion

- **Fills:** a row's hover, a selected row and a sort button change fill on the
  rollover's clocks: in on fast-02, back on fast-01, standard-productive. A
  selected row takes a 10% wash of the text color. Hover steps off the row's
  fill, over a selection or a stripe too. The toolbar search has no fill at
  rest; it takes a 5% wash on hover, on focus and once typed in, on the same
  clocks.
- **Sort icon:** a sortable header's icon is `SortDirection`, one icon for none,
  ascending and descending. A click plays its change: it shrinks to a line, then
  regrows. The unsorted icon shows only while the button is hovered or focused;
  a sorted column's always shows.
- **Expanding row:** the details push the rows below open, as one layout change.
  The height opens through a soft edge of up to 16px, on moderate-01 (160ms) for
  a 24 to 120px row, entrance-productive. The content moves 8px down into place
  over fast-02, starting once the height is half open. Shutting is one step
  faster, fast-02 (120ms), exit-productive, and the content holds still. A
  toggle mid-move turns round from where it is. The content is clipped, never
  scaled.
- **Chevron:** `ChevronDownUpToggle`, down shut and up open, on the rollover: a
  wash of the text color, 8% on hover and 16% on press.
- **Expand all:** rows that open or shut together are one motion, on one clock
  from the total travel, capped at moderate-02 (220ms) opening and 160ms
  shutting.
- **Reveal:** `DataTableReveal`'s header, column widths and selection column are
  the real table from the first frame and never move. While loading, a skeleton
  body lies over the rows' space. When the rows land, they fade in place, 30ms
  apart, as the skeleton dissolves over them. Until then the sort buttons are
  disabled and select-all is off.
- **Reflow:** in `DataTableReveal`, rows with ids reflow when they change: a
  filter, a search or a sort. Leaving rows fade where they are, on fast-01. Then
  the rows that stay and the body's height move as one layout change, on the
  farthest travel's clock. Then arriving rows fade in, staggered. Selection
  follows each row's id. A new `pageKey` is not a reflow: another page's rows
  are simply there.

## Reduced motion

Fills change at once. The sort icon changes at once. Rows open and shut at once,
with no soft edge; the content fades in on a shorter clock. A reflow keeps only
its fades, on a shorter clock.
