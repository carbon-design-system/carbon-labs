## Overview

A date picker is a text field for a date with, in `single` and `range`, a
calendar under it. `DatePicker` holds the state and the calendar; its children
are `DatePickerInput`s, one for `simple` and `single`, two for `range`. Prop
names follow Carbon React's `DatePicker`, but this is its own implementation and
not every Carbon prop is supported: see [Component API](#component-api).

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.SingleWithCalendar} />

### Simple date picker

`datePickerType="simple"` renders the text field with no calendar and no
calendar icon.

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.Simple} />

### Single date picker

`datePickerType="single"` pairs the text field with a calendar. Focusing or
clicking the field opens the calendar; choosing a day fills the field.

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.SingleWithCalendar} />

### Range date picker

`datePickerType="range"` takes two `DatePickerInput`s. The first holds the start
date and the second the end date. Until the end is chosen, the calendar shades
the days between the start and the focused day.

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.RangeWithCalendar} />

### Skeleton state

`DatePickerSkeleton` is the loading placeholder. `range` draws two fields.

### AI label

`DatePickerInput` takes an AI label through `decorator`. An `AILabel` is drawn
at its `mini` size.

## Usage

```tsx
import { DatePicker, DatePickerInput } from '@carbon-labs/react-date-picker';
import '@carbon-labs/react-date-picker/scss/date-picker.scss';

<DatePicker datePickerType="single" onChange={(dates) => console.log(dates)}>
  <DatePickerInput id="date" labelText="Date" />
</DatePicker>;
```

The component uses the `Temporal` API for dates and needs `Temporal` in the
global scope. The package doesn't include a polyfill.

## Motion

None. The calendar appears and disappears at once, and its styles have no
transitions.

## Reduced motion

The same as above: there is no motion to reduce.

## Keyboard

- **Focus on a field** opens the calendar (`single` and `range`).
- **Tab** from a field opens the calendar if it is closed, and moves focus into
  it if it is open.
- **Arrow keys** move the focused day.
- **Page Up / Page Down** move a month back or forward.
- **Home / End** move to the start or end of the week.
- **Enter** selects the focused day.
- **Escape** closes the calendar.
- **Tab** from the calendar closes it and moves focus to what follows the date
  picker. **Shift+Tab** from the calendar returns focus to the field last
  focused.

When the calendar opens with no date chosen, the focused day is today.

## Accessibility

- Each field's label is tied to it with `for`. `required` adds an asterisk to
  the label and sets `required` on the field.
- `invalid` sets `aria-invalid`. The error, warning or helper text, whichever
  shows, is the field's `aria-describedby`.
- The calendar is `role="grid"` with the label "Calendar". Each day is a button
  named for its full date, such as "January 1, 2026". The chosen day has
  `aria-pressed`, today has `aria-current="date"`, and days outside `minDate`
  and `maxDate` are disabled buttons.
- The month buttons are named "Previous month" and "Next month".
- The calendar icon is a button named by `iconDescription` and left out of the
  Tab order.
- Month and weekday names are in English only.

## Known issues

- Typed text isn't kept: the field shows only a date chosen in the calendar or a
  `value` passed to `DatePickerInput`. In `simple` this means the field can't be
  filled.
- In `simple`, Tab doesn't move focus out of the field.
- `value` on `DatePicker` doesn't choose a date or fill the field. See
  [`value`](#date-picker-value).
- `DatePickerProps` declares `appendTo`, `allowInput` and `locale`, but the
  component doesn't read them. They are left out of the tables below.

### Date picker `dateFormat`

`dateFormat` sets how a chosen date is written in the field. The default is
`m/d/Y`. The tokens are:

- `Y`: four-digit year
- `y`: two-digit year
- `m`: month, two digits (`01`-`12`)
- `n`: month, no leading zero (`1`-`12`)
- `d`: day, two digits (`01`-`31`)
- `j`: day, no leading zero (`1`-`31`)

```tsx
<DatePicker dateFormat="Y-m-d">
  <DatePickerInput
    id="date-picker-format"
    placeholder="yyyy-mm-dd"
    labelText="ISO format"
  />
</DatePicker>
```

### Date picker `minDate` and `maxDate`

Days before `minDate` or after `maxDate` are disabled in the calendar. Both
limits are inclusive. Each takes `mm/dd/yyyy` (or `m/d/yyyy`) or `yyyy-mm-dd`,
whatever `dateFormat` is.

```tsx
<DatePicker minDate="01/01/2024" maxDate="12/31/2024">
  <DatePickerInput id="date" placeholder="mm/dd/yyyy" />
</DatePicker>
```

### Date picker `value`

`value` on `DatePicker` is stored on the state machine but doesn't choose a date
or fill the field. `value` on `DatePickerInput` sets the field's text, and that
text is shown while it is set.

## How it is built

`DatePicker` runs on the date picker state machine in
`@carbon-labs/primitives/date-picker`, which the web component date picker also
uses. The machine holds the mode, the chosen dates, the month in view and the
focused day, and maps keys to its events. The styles come from the same package.
