# Dropdown

A field that opens a floating list. `Dropdown` chooses one option, and
`ComboBox` is a Dropdown you can type into, its list filtered as you type.
`MultiSelect` chooses many, with a check column and a count tag in the field,
and `FilterableMultiSelect` puts its list under the ComboBox's field. They take
Carbon's props. The field is the text input's, with its label, status mark and
one message line. The list is the Menu's surface, at the field's width, five and
a half rows tall; it flips to stay on screen. `type="inline"` puts the label
beside a quiet field. The skeletons are the forms while their values load.

## Motion

- **Snap:** the list snaps open from the press point, or from the field's center
  when a key opens it, clamped to the list's near edge.
- **Fold:** the list closes by folding its far edge back into where it came
  from. It keeps what it shows while it leaves.
- **Highlight:** the kit's highlight marker. Hover jumps it, keys glide it, and
  a held key jumps it. The first arrival grows in; every later one is simply
  there. It stays on the active option when the pointer leaves.
- **Press:** a held option's marker insets 1px.
- **Chevron:** the chevron turns while the list shows. On the Dropdown and the
  MultiSelect, the field's hover plays it; on the combo boxes, its own button's
  hover does.
- **Filter:** in the combo boxes, a key after a pause changes the list out then
  in. The options leaving fade where they are. Then the list's height and the
  options that stay move as one. Then the new options fade in. The marker comes
  back still on the active option.
- **Typing burst:** keys less than 200ms apart land at once. The rows and the
  height are simply there, and the marker stays still.
- **Reorder:** with `selectionFeedback="top"`, a choice goes out, the list makes
  room at the top, and it comes back in there. A key's choice moves at once; a
  pointer's waits until the pointer leaves the row.
- **Count tag:** the multi-selects' count snaps in with the first choice and out
  with the last. Its number rolls up as items are added and down as they go.
- **Check:** a multi-select's check column is the kit's Checkbox, which draws
  its check.
- **Soft edge:** the list scrolls on the kit's Scroller.
- **Outline ladder, error border, message line and status mark:** the text
  input's. A choice is one act, so a status shows at once.
- **Late status:** the ComboBox's errors wait for a pause in typing or for blur,
  as the text input's. `lateErrors={false}` shows them at once.

## Reduced motion

The list is there at once and gone at once. A filter or a reorder only fades,
still out then in, on half the clock. The highlight does not travel: it fades in
on the new option as it fades out of the old, on half the clock. The count tag
and the check change at once. The outline and the error border change on half of
fast-01; the message line and the status mark change at once. A rising status in
the ComboBox still waits for the pause or blur.
