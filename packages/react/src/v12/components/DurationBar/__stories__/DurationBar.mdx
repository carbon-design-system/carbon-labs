# DurationBar

A duration on a shared scale, drawn by the Slider: the `track` shape at `sm`,
its fill the duration, with dots every 100ms, so a column of bars reads as one
scale. With `onChange` it is editable: drag the knob, press the track, use the
keys (5ms; Shift, 50ms) or type in the number field. Values snap to 5ms and stay
within 0 and `max` (default 1000). Without `onChange` it is a fixed value: a bar
to the value, no knob and no hover, a meter to assistive tech. `reference` marks
a second value, such as the last saved one. `hideNumber` hides the number. There
is no disabled state: a duration that can't be edited reads only.

## Motion

The motion is the Slider's, on the track. A fixed bar has none.

- **Glide:** a key or a click sends the knob from where it is to the new value,
  on standard-productive. Its duration comes from the distance. A held key's
  repeats move at once.
- **Press:** a press on the track starts the knob toward the pressed point. The
  release lands there.
- **Drag:** the knob follows the pointer exactly and squashes against the ends.
- **Hover and press:** hover grows the knob 2px. A pointer press takes back 1px.
  A press with no hover, a key or a touch, insets it 1px. On fast-01.
- **Fill:** rides the knob's live center on every frame. On hover it steps
  toward the knob's color and grows thicker, on fast-02, standard-productive.
- **Dots:** the dot under the pointer grows, and a press within 12px of a dot
  glides the knob there.

## Reduced motion

No glide: the knob moves to the new value and fades in there, on half the clock.
There is no press reach. The knob's hover and press keep their clock.
