# EasingGraph

A cubic-bezier as a graph: progress up, time across, on a quarter grid with the
linear diagonal for reference. The curve is 3px solid, a `reference` curve 3px
dotted behind it, the handles' arms 2px dashed. Without `onChange` it is read
only, with the four numbers as text. With `onChange`, the two handles drag or
take the arrow keys, and the four numbers edit as one field in four segments.
Handles stay inside the unit box, so a curve never overshoots. A typed number
outside 0 to 1 is flagged and not applied.

## Motion

- **Handle ring:** each handle is a 2px ring on the plot's fill, on the house
  rollover. The ring steps up in contrast: into hover on fast-02, to the press
  color at once, back on fast-01.
- **Handle press:** pressed or dragged, the handle grows from 12px to 18px on
  fast-01, and shrinks back on fast-02. The pointer is captured, so the press
  holds while the handle follows it.
- **Arm:** the grabbed handle's arm turns solid, in the press color, its color
  on fast-02.
- **Curve:** follows the handles and the numbers as they change, with no tween.
- **Keys:** the arrows move a handle 0.01, or 0.1 with Shift: left and right its
  x, up and down its y.

## Reduced motion

The ring and the handle's size change at once. The arm's color keeps its clock.
The curve is the same as with full motion.
