# EmptyState

What a region shows when it has nothing to show: first use, no results, an
error, no access. It has a visual, a required `title`, a `subtitle`, an `action`
and a `link`, and no words of its own. The visual is your own image, a still
32px `icon`, or the drawing for a `kind`; `EmptyStateArt` is that drawing alone.
`context` is the region it sits in, a page, a table's place, a panel or a tile,
and sets the size and the alignment. With no context, it is a panel on a framed
surface and a page elsewhere. It has no surface of its own.

## Motion

- **No motion of its own:** it is simply there. The drawings and the icon are
  still.
- **Content swap:** a swap to or from the empty state is its region's content
  swap, out then in. The old content leaves fully before the new content comes
  in, so no frame shows both. In the `ListCleared` story, the region plays it.
- **Rollover:** the action and the link take the kit Button's and Link's
  rollover. `timeScale` multiplies their clock.

## Reduced motion

The empty state has nothing of its own to change. The region's content swap is a
snap: the new content is there on the frame the old one goes. The action and the
link follow their own reduced rules.
