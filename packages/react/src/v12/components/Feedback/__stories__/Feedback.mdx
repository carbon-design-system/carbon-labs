# Feedback

The kit's shared feedback parts, which other v12 components build on:

- `Spinner`: the loader, in six sizes, neutral or hero.
- `StatusMark`: a status's icon, with the hand-off from pending to a result.
- `CountPill`: a count whose changing digits roll.
- `SkeletonText`, `SkeletonPlaceholder` and `SkeletonOf`: loading states,
  pulsing on the skeleton wave; `SkeletonOf` draws a component as its own
  skeleton.

## Reduced motion

Nothing turns: three still dots stand in for the spinner. A status is there at
once, its glyph drawn, with no hand-off. A count's new figure fades in place on
the reduced clock, with no roll. Skeletons do not pulse; their blocks rest at
their peak.
