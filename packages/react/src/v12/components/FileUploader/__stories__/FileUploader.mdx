# FileUploader

Carbon's file uploader, with its props. `FileUploader` is a title, a
description, an add button and the files it has added, all with one
`filenameStatus`; its ref clears them (`clearFiles()`). For an uploader the app
builds itself, the parts are `FileUploaderButton`, `FileUploaderDropContainer`,
`FileUploaderItem` and `FileUploaderSkeleton`. Each file is an item on the quiet
surface with one status slot: a progress mark while it uploads and once it is
complete, and a remove button to edit. With `progress`, a known size, the mark
is a ring that fills by it; without, it is the Spinner. `arrive={false}` lists a
file with no motion, as for files already there on load.

## Motion

- **Arrive:** an item opens its place in the list as it mounts, as a disclosure:
  on its travel's step, the content following from half the open. What is below
  moves with it.
- **Arrival stagger:** items added in one commit open in document order on a
  stagger. Its step comes from the items' pitch, and the last starts within the
  stagger window.
- **Status hand-off:** uploading and complete are one mark, the ProgressRing at
  16px. Complete is that mark changing in place: the ring completes, then it
  shrinks 4px, the success disc grows from 4px under its size, and the check
  draws on.
- **Face swap:** a change between the progress mark and the remove button is a
  content swap in place. The old face fades fully out, then the new fades in,
  with no travel.
- **Error:** an invalid item's error opens under the name as its own disclosure
  body, so the item grows. The new face waits for that move to land. The warning
  mark plays its motion icon once it has landed.
- **Leave:** a removed item leaves a copy where it stood, and the copy's place
  closes, one step faster, its content held still. Items removed together each
  rise 8px and fade out in order, then close as one motion.
- **Drag-over:** with a file held over the drop zone, the rollover's hover glow
  follows the file and a 2px blue ring fades in inside the edge, in on
  `fast-02`, out on `fast-01`. Moving onto a child of the zone does not end it.
- **Rollover:** the drop zone, the remove button and the add button take the
  kit's rollover.
- **Label roll:** the add button's label changes to the chosen file's name, or
  "N files", through RollText, so only the parts that change roll.
  `disableLabelChanges` keeps the label.

## Reduced motion

No travel and no stagger: an item is there, or gone, at once, and so is its
error. The faces snap and the marks do not beat. The ring and the glow on the
drop zone are there or gone at once. The add button's label swaps in place.
