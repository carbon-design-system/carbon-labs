# Form layout

One layout for settings, create and edit forms. `FormLayout` is the form and its
widest: narrow 320px, medium 640px by default, wide 960px or full. `as="div"`
puts it inside another form. `FormSection` is a title, a description 8px under
it and its fields 24px below, in a fieldset. `FormRow` puts fields side by side
from a 480px form, 16px apart, and stacks them under it; `columns` sets how
many. Sections are 48px apart and fields 32px, the room a field's message line
opens into. A layout's or a section's `disabled` and `readOnly` reach every
field in it: the kit's fields read them with `useFormFieldState`, and a disabled
section also disables any other control in it. `UnsavedChangesBar` holds Save
and Discard at the bottom of its scroll while the form has changes, with
`useDirtyForm` to track them. `SavingStatus` shows where a save stands.

## Motion

- **None:** a form holds still while it is filled in. Rows restack at once when
  the form crosses 480px. Each field keeps its own motion.
- **Save:** the unsaved changes bar arrives on the first change and holds still
  after. It snaps in from its bottom edge, opaque from 4px. Its content moves
  8px up into place from the first frame and lands just after the bar. The clock
  is the step for the bar's height, on entrance-productive. Leaving, its top
  edge folds back down a step faster, on exit-productive, and the content holds
  still.
- **Save swap:** the bar's message and actions swap in place, for the discard
  question and a failed save. The count rolls to each new number.
- **Saving status:** the mark hands off from one status to the next. The spinner
  shrinks 4px, the disc grows in, and the check or slash draws on, with no
  fades. The label swaps in place, out then in, on the content swap's clocks.

## Reduced motion

The layout has nothing to change. The bar only fades, at its final geometry, on
the reduced clock. Its swaps change at once. The saving status's mark and label
change at once.
