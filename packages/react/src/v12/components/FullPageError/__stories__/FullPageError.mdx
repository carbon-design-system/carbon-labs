# FullPageError

A whole page that says something went wrong: a 403, a 404, or a custom error.
Its props are the Carbon Products FullPageError's.

<Canvas of={FullPageErrorStories.Default} />

## Motion

- The label's arrow draws: its line from the top, down and right, and its head
  grows from the tip before the line ends.
- The error (label and title) fades in as the head draws; then the description,
  actions and art fade in.
- A new error on a mounted page swaps out, then in.
- Held still while it is off screen.

## Reduced motion

No drawing: the page fades in at once, on the reduced clock.
