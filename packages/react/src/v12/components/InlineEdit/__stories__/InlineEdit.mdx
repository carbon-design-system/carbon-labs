# InlineEdit

A value shown as text that turns into its field in place. The view is the text,
a slot for a status mark and an edit button. The field is a text input, a text
area or a select, with save and cancel buttons. Press the view or the edit
button to open the field. Enter saves; in a text area, Shift+Enter breaks a
line. Escape cancels. Focus goes to the field, its text selected, and back to
the edit button on save or cancel. `validate` checks the draft on save. A save
that returns a promise shows saving, and a rejection reopens the field with its
message. Read-only and disabled values show a lock or a crossed pencil, with
`reason` in its tooltip.

## Motion

- **Content swap in place:** the text and the field swap in place, out then in,
  by fade only. The old face fades out on fast-01, then the new one fades in on
  fast-02.
- **Layout move:** when the faces differ in size, the box holds its old size,
  then moves to the new one on the step for its travel, on standard-productive.
  The new face arrives once it lands.
- **Mark snap:** save and cancel snap in as the field arrives. Each is opaque at
  half size, then grows to full on fast-02, entrance-productive.
- **Status hand-off:** a save that returns a promise closes the field at once,
  with a spinner beside the new value. When it resolves, the spinner hands off
  to a check: it shrinks, the disc grows and the check draws on. A save that
  returns nothing shows the check alone. The check holds for `savedHold` (1500ms
  by default), then fades on fast-02.
- **Validation:** an error keeps the field open and shows on the field's message
  line without waiting for a pause. While it shows, each change checks the draft
  again.
- **Quiet hover:** the view takes the rollover's even wash, flat on press. The
  edit button fades in on hover or focus, on fast-01. Where there is no hover,
  it always shows.

## Reduced motion

The faces swap at once, and the box takes its new size at once. Save and cancel
are there at once. The status mark is simply there, and the check goes at once
after its hold. `reduced` overrides the preference.
