# JobsTray

Background jobs in a panel from the header, with no Carbon counterpart. Each job
has a title, a ProgressBar, a StatusIndicator, and Cancel or Retry. A job is
`queued`, `running`, `finished`, `failed` or `cancelled`; its `progress` is 0 to
100, and a running job with none is indeterminate. The host owns `open` and
`jobs`. A press outside, or Escape, calls `onClickOutside`, and Escape puts
focus back on the trigger. `JobsTrayIndicator` is the trigger's face: put it in
the header's IconButton, and name the button with the count and the status
(`jobsStatus`, `jobsStatusLabel`).

## Motion

- **Open:** the floating snap from its trigger, timed by size. A tray over 320px
  takes the surface rule's clocks. The presence fade runs with it. Closing, the
  far edge folds back.
- **Progress:** each job's bar retargets as progress comes in, and travels only
  on an update. Done, the bar sits full; queued, empty.
- **Status:** the StatusIndicator hands off from pending to the job's end.
- **Arrive:** a job added while the tray is open opens its place.
- **Clear finished:** each ended job's place shuts. Once the list is empty, the
  empty text comes in after the places have shut.
- **Trigger mark:** the activity icon carries a status mark in its corner:
  pending while any job is at work, then failed if any ended failed, else done.
  The mark stays while ended jobs are in the tray; once cleared, the plain icon
  is back. The mark comes in, changes and goes on its own motion.
- **Count:** the jobs still working, on the CountPill, whose digits roll.
- **Toast:** a job that ends while the tray is closed shows one quiet toast,
  grouped with the others, and never again for that run. A success leaves after
  5 seconds; a failure stays. A retry starts a new run. It toasts only where a
  `ToastStack` is above it, and not with `toast={false}`.

## Reduced motion

The tray fades in place. Places open and shut at once. The trigger's mark is
there at once, with pending as still dots. The bar and the count take their own
reduced motion.
