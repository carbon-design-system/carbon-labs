# JumpTo

A floating pill back to a point in a scrolled container. It shows while its
target is out of view by more than `threshold`, and hides when the target is
back. It sits at the top or the bottom center of the view, with an arrow toward
the target. A preset sets the target, the words and where the target lands:
Latest (the live edge, counting what arrives), Unread, Top, Selection or
Current. The pill is the kit Button, tonal, and takes no room in the layout. A
count is announced in a polite live region. `useJumpTo` is the same without the
pill, for a host that draws its own control.

## Motion

- **4px arrival:** the pill arrives from 4px short of its measured width and
  height, about its center, on fast-02 with the entrance-snap curve, as it fades
  in. It leaves to the same size on fast-01, exit-productive. When the target
  moves to the other side, the pill leaves, then arrives at its new place.
- **Scroll glide:** a jump glides the container to the target, on a clock set by
  the distance, with the standard-productive curve. Past two views, it first
  moves to one view short of the target, then glides the rest. A reader's own
  wheel, touch, key or press stops the glide where it is.
- **Stick to end:** with Latest, a view at the end stays there as content grows.
  A reader's own scroll toward the start lets go once it settles.
- **Keyboard:** Enter or Space on the pill jumps and moves focus to the target,
  or to the container. A pointer press leaves focus where it is.

## Reduced motion

The jump lands at once, with no glide. The pill only fades, in and out.
`reduced` overrides the preference.
