# Loading

`Loading` and `InlineLoading`, with Carbon's props, on the kit's one spinner.

<Canvas of={LoadingStories.Default} />

## Motion

- **Start:** the arc draws on, its tail lengthening back from its head; an
  overlay fades up on that clock.
- **Stop:** `active={false}`: the tail catches up with the head, and the spinner
  shrinks to half and fades in its last frames.
- **Gate:** `delay` and `minDuration` show nothing for the first `delay` ms,
  then keep the spinner at least `minDuration` ms once it shows. Both default to
  0, as in Carbon.
- **InlineLoading:** a change of status changes the mark in place: the spinner
  shrinks, the result's disc grows, then its check or slash draws on. The text
  swaps out, then in, on the same clocks.

<Canvas of={LoadingStories.Inline} />

## Reduced motion

Nothing turns: three still dots stand in for the arc, there or gone at once. The
overlay fades on the reduced clock. InlineLoading's mark changes at once and its
text snaps; the words carry the state.
