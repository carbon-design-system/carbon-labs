# Menu

A floating list of actions and choices, with Carbon's API: `MenuItem`,
`MenuItemSelectable`, `MenuItemRadioGroup`, `MenuItemGroup` and
`MenuItemDivider`, and a MenuItem's own MenuItems as a submenu. `MenuButton` and
`OverflowMenu` open one from a button, as SplitButton does; `x` and `y` place
one at a point, as a context menu. One highlight serves the pointer and the
keys, and labels take its color where it covers them. With `inline`, the menu is
drawn in place, with no portal, placement, snap or close.

## Corners

8px by default, with rows at a field's inset, on every trigger: a pill
MenuButton opens an 8px menu. `corners="full"`, or `KitDefaults corners="full"`,
draws the pill, its rows half a row's height in. `corners="square"` has none.

## Motion

- **Open:** the frame grows about its cause: the press that opened it, else the
  trigger's center, as from the keyboard. `openFrom="trigger"` on a button
  always starts at its center.
- **Content:** the list slides out of the frame on y only.
- **Fade:** it fades up over the first part of the open and out over the last
  part of the close: 40ms on a clock of 220ms or less, else 80ms.
  `exitFade={false}` folds back opaque.
- **Close:** the far edge folds back into the point it grew from, the list
  still. It is gone on the fold's last frame. Reopened while it leaves, it turns
  round from where it is.
- **Highlight:** its first arrival in an open menu grows in from the row's
  center. Under the pointer it jumps from row to row. The arrow keys glide it,
  timed by travel. It shrinks back and fades when the pointer leaves the list.
- **Danger:** on a danger row the highlight turns to the danger button's color,
  on the glide's clock.
- **Press:** the highlight insets 1px while a row is held.
- **Submenu:** one level deep, marked by a chevron. It opens beside its row
  after 100ms of hover, or on → or Enter, and snaps from the row. ← and Escape
  go back.
- **Chevron:** on MenuButton, the chevron turns while the menu shows.
- **Keyboard:** ↑ and ↓ wrap; Home, End and typeahead move too. Disabled rows
  are skipped unless they have a `disabledReason`. A radio group's `shortcuts`
  win over typeahead. A trigger opens on the first row with Enter, Space or ↓,
  and on the last with ↑.
- **Cast shadow:** off by default. `shadow` turns it on; it grows and folds with
  the frame.

## Reduced motion

The menu and its submenus are there at once and gone at once: no growth, slide,
fold or fade. The highlight does not travel: it fades in on the new row as it
fades out of the old, on half the clock. A menu placed again while open moves at
once.
