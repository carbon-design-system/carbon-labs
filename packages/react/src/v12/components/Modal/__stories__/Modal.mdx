# Modal

A centred dialog over a dimmed page, with Carbon's props. `Modal` is one piece
and controlled: every way of closing calls `onRequestClose`, and only `open`
closes it. `ComposedModal` is its shell, built from `ModalHeader`, `ModalBody`
and `ModalFooter`; it closes itself unless `onClose` returns `false`. A passive
modal has no footer. The body scrolls when it overflows, and the footer's
primary button can show a loading status.

## Motion

- **Overlay:** the dim fades in over moderate-01 (entrance-productive) and out
  over fast-02 (exit-productive), from wherever it is.
- **Snap arrival:** the frame grows to its place in the centre, from 92% of its
  box on each axis, or 16px in on a side where that is more. It runs over
  fast-02 on entrance-snap. The content moves in with it on y.
- **Cursor origin:** it grows from its top or bottom edge facing the press that
  opened it, at the press's x. Opened from a key, it grows from the focused
  launcher's centre the same way. With no launcher, it grows from its own
  centre.
- **Pull-back:** leaving, the frame shrinks back to the same start, toward the
  same origin, over fast-01 on exit-pullback, fading as it goes. Reopened while
  it leaves, it turns round from where it is.
- **Page recede and blur:** the page behind draws back to 99% about the
  viewport's centre, under one even blur, on the overlay's clocks. `backdrop`
  leaves either out.
- **Content swap:** a new `swapKey` on `ModalBody` swaps its content. The old
  content leaves 16px up over fast-01, fading, then the new content enters from
  16px below over fast-02. The height holds through the out, then glides with
  the in, and the modal re-centres. The primary button's loading label swaps the
  same way, its width gliding.
- **Loading:** the mark at the primary button's end changes in place, from the
  spinner to the check or the error mark. `onLoadingSuccess` is called 1.5s
  after `finished`.
- **Cast shadow:** off by default; `shadow` turns it on. It grows and pulls back
  with the frame. While the pointer is over or near the modal, the shadow falls
  away from it, and it eases back to rest when the pointer leaves.
- **Focus:** focus moves in on open and Tab wraps inside. Focus goes back to
  `launcherButtonRef`, else to what had it, as the modal starts to leave.

## Reduced motion

The overlay and the surface only fade, at their final geometry, on half the
clock. The blur is there at once, and the page doesn't scale. A content swap
shows the new content at once, with no fade or glide. The shadow's light moves
at once.
