# Notification

Inline, toast and actionable notifications, and the callout, with Carbon's
props: the six kinds, `title`, `subtitle`, `caption`, `lowContrast`,
`hideCloseButton`, `role`, a toast's `timeout` and `onClose`. A plain surface
whose outline, in the status color, says the kind. `variant` picks outlined,
inverse or tonal; by default errors and actionable notifications are inverse,
the rest outlined. `ToastStack` is the one place toasts live, and `useToasts`
shows and closes them from anywhere inside it. `StaticNotification` is the
callout's deprecated name.

## Motion

- **Arrive:** the snap from the top center. The container grows, the content
  slides on y, and nothing else moves. `from` grows it from a control instead.
  With `arrive={false}` it is part of the page from the start, as a callout is.
- **Leave:** the far edge folds back into the top center it grew from.
- **Inline place:** an inline notification opens its place in the page as it
  arrives and closes it as it leaves, on one clock. What is below it follows.
- **Status mark:** the mark plays its motion icon once it has landed. With
  `icon`, an action icon loads in with its status; with `pending`, it shows its
  spinner until the kind changes. A change of kind is a match cut.
- **Update in place:** new words, from an update by id or a group's next member,
  swap out, then in. No frame shows both. The status turns on the empty card
  between them, and so does a change of height, timed by its travel.
- **Timer:** a toast's timeout you can see, as a ring round the close button or
  a bar under the text (`timer`). Hover or focus pauses it.
- **Buttons:** the close and action buttons take the rollover. The close
  button's icon is a motion icon.
- **Focus:** closed with focus inside, focus moves to the next toast, or back
  where it came from.

### Toast stack

- **Deck:** the newest toast in front, the next two behind it, each 8px below
  the one in front and inset 6px a side. Their content is faded out, and each
  further back is dimmer.
- **Front change:** with `frontChange="swap"` (the default) the front stays one
  surface. A new toast turns it to its own outline, fill and height while the
  text crossfades in place, and the old front steps down the stack behind it.
  The snap plays only into an empty deck and out of a last card. With `snap`,
  every arrival and exit snaps.
- **One move:** every change in the deck starts on the same frame and runs on
  one clock and one curve.
- **Open:** hover or focus deals the deck into a list, newest at the top.
  Leaving closes it after a beat. On touch, a tap opens it and a tap elsewhere
  closes it.
- **Cap:** `cap` toasts at once (3). One more pushes the oldest to the back,
  where it fades out. An error, or a toast asking for action, is never pushed
  out.
- **Missed:** a toast that times out or is pushed out is counted (`onMissed`) on
  the frame it starts to go. A toast the person closes just leaves.
- **Group:** `show` with a `group` already showing merges into that toast. Its
  count rolls up, its text and kind become the latest, and it comes to the
  front.
- **Burst:** of toasts shown at once, only the newest arrives. The ones behind
  it fade in at their places once it has landed.
- **Swipe:** a toast can be swiped right, off the screen's edge. Let go early,
  it settles back.
- **Stillness:** a toast that takes the place of one with the same mark does not
  replay its icon.
- **Cast shadow:** off by default; `shadow` turns it on. Only the front casts in
  a closed deck, and every card does in the open list.

## Reduced motion

A notification is there at once and gone at once, with no growth, fold or fade.
An inline notification's place opens and closes at once, and so does a change of
height. An update shows the new words on the same frame the old ones go. The
status mark is simply there, with no beat. In a stack, the deck's fades and
resizes run on the reduced clock, with no travel and no deal, and a swipe does
not follow the pointer.
