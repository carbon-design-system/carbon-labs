# NotificationsPanel

Carbon for IBM Products' notifications panel, prop for prop: a floating panel
from the header's bell, its notifications grouped into Today, Yesterday and
Previous. Each row has a status mark, when it came, a title, a description with
Read more past two lines, and a link. Unread rows carry a blue dot cut out of
the mark, and a semibold title. The header holds Dismiss all, Do not disturb and
settings; View all is the foot. The host owns `open` and `data`. A press
outside, not on the trigger, or Escape calls `onClickOutside`, and Escape puts
focus back on the trigger. `NotificationsBell` is the trigger's icon: pass it
the panel's Do not disturb state, and the same state back as
`doNotDisturbDefaultToggled`, so the panel opens as it was left.

## Motion

- **Open:** the floating snap from its trigger, timed by size. The frame grows
  from the trigger while the content moves down with the opening, landing just
  after the frame.
- **Close:** the far edge pulls back into the trigger. Reopened mid-exit, it
  turns round.
- **Dismiss:** a dismissed row's place shuts, one step faster as an exit, while
  its content holds still and the soft edge closes over it, with no fade. What
  is below closes up with it in one move. A group's title goes with its last
  row.
- **Dismiss all:** the titles and rows leave on a stagger faster than the
  entrance, each rising 8px and fading out. Then the list resizes to the empty
  state, which comes in after it.
- **Arrive:** a notification added while the panel is open opens its place on
  its travel's step under the soft edge. Its content comes down 8px into it from
  half the open over `fast-02`, with no fade. Its status mark plays its beat
  once it has landed.
- **Read more:** the description's place opens on its travel's step and shuts
  one step faster, under the soft edge. Its lines are already there, so the text
  is uncovered in place. The clamp comes back once the place has closed. The
  label rolls, and the chevron turns.
- **Do not disturb:** the header's toggle and the bell slash on the icon's own
  motion. Turned on, the pip goes and the slash draws in. Turned off, the slash
  retracts and the bell rings, then the pip is back. Arrivals keep coming.
- **View all:** the whole footer cell takes the ghost Button's rollover. Its
  count rolls.
- **Rollover:** a row that is a control (`onNotificationClick`), Read more and
  the link take the kit's rollover.

## Reduced motion

The panel fades in place. Places open and close at once. Dismiss all and an
arrival's content are fades on the reduced clock, with no travel. The bell's
drawings change at once, and Read more's label swaps in place.
