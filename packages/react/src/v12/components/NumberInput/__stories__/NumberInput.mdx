# NumberInput

A field for a number, with − and + steppers inset at its trailing edge. It is
the text input's field: its label, outline, status mark and one message line. On
a step the number rolls to its next value. `NumberInput` takes Carbon's props,
plus `corners`, `frame`, `variant`, `lateErrors`, `typingPauseMs`,
`messagePlacement`, `statusBeat`, `timeScale` and the AI fill state.
`type="text"` parses and formats the number for a locale. `NumberInputSkeleton`
is the field while its value loads.

## Motion

- **Roll:** a step rolls the number to its next value: up as it rises, down as
  it falls, only the digits that change. It runs on moderate-01 with
  standard-productive; a roll that interrupts one already moving carries on at
  speed. A step is a stepper, ↑ or ↓ in the field, or a `value` set from outside
  that isn't the one the field reported. The roll sits over the input's own
  text, which stays a real input. Typing never rolls, and neither does a step
  held at a bound.
- **Outline ladder:** the text input's. Hover, press and focus step the outline.
- **Error border:** an error settles in once, with the text input's border.
- **Message line:** helper, warning and error share one line and swap in place,
  out then in.
- **Status mark:** the warning or error mark sits before the steppers and swaps
  as the status changes. `statusBeat` plays its icon once as it arrives.
- **Late status:** an error that rises while typing, a number out of range say,
  waits for a pause (`typingPauseMs`, default 1000ms) or blur. One that falls
  shows on the keystroke. `lateErrors={false}` shows every status at once.
- **Rollover:** the steppers are ghost icon buttons, with the rollover's press
  shrink and no hover expand. Each icon plays on its button's hover.
- **Tooltip:** the two steppers share one tooltip. Moving from − to + puts it
  there at once, with its text changing in place.
- **AI fill:** a fill and a revert swap the value; they never roll.

## Reduced motion

The number changes at once, with no roll. The outline and the error border
change on half of fast-01. The message line, the status mark, the steppers'
release, the tooltip and an AI fill change at once. A rising status still waits
for the pause or blur.
