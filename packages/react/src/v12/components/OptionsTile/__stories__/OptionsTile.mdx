# Options tile

A tile for one group of settings. The heading is a button that holds the
chevron, the title and a one-line `summary` of what the settings are set to.
`enabled` adds a switch over the heading's end that turns the group on or off;
`onChange` hears it. The body opens to show the settings, its children. Without
children the tile doesn't open, and the heading is plain text. `invalid`, `warn`
and `locked` put a status, with its text, in the summary's place, in that order.
Open, the summary goes, but a status stays. Locked makes the switch read-only.
`open` is the initial state, followed whenever it changes, and `onToggle` hears
each toggle. `size` sets the heading: lg 48px (default) or xl 64px.

## Motion

The body is the shared disclosure.

- **Open and shut:** the body opens as the summary's line shuts, as one swap on
  one clock: the step of the longer travel, on standard-productive. Shutting,
  the line opens as the body shuts. Tiles that change in one commit move as one
  motion.
- **Settings in:** opening, the settings come down 8px from the opening's first
  frame, over one duration token longer than the opening.
- **Sheet:** open, a hairline sits atop the body and holds still. The body's
  sheet runs to the tile's bottom over the heading's lower part. It fades in and
  out at the motion's ends. The heading's lower corners follow the body on its
  clock and curve, so they never snap.
- **Summary fade:** the summary only fades, in place, over fast-01. Opening, it
  is gone before anything moves over it. Shutting, it comes back once the
  heading has landed.
- **Summary words:** new words swap out, then in. They swap in place, or, when
  the switch changed them, 8px the way the knob went.
- **Rollover:** the tiles' quiet rollover on the heading, flush, with no growth
  past the tile and no press shrink. With a switch, the heading shows a ring
  instead of a fill.
- **Switch:** the kit's Toggle, with its own glide.
- **Chevron:** down when shut, up when open. The heading is its motion icon
  frame.

`timeScale` multiplies the disclosure, the rollover, the summary swap and the
switch.

## Reduced motion

The disclosure opens and shuts at once, and the settings fade in on the reduced
clock. The summary changes at once, and its swap is a snap. The switch fades
rather than glides.
