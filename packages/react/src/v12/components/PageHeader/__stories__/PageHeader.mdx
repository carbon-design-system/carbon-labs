# Page header

The top of a page: a breadcrumb, a title, a subtitle, `children`, tags, page
actions, an action bar and tabs (`navigation`). It sticks to the top of its
scroll container, `pageHeaderOffset` below it. Past 16px of scroll it collapses
to one 48px row of the breadcrumb, the title, the page actions and the action
bar, then the tabs. Back at the top it expands. `collapseHeader` holds it
collapsed, and `hasCollapseHeaderToggle` adds a button for it. Its layout
follows its own width, not the window's. Below 672px the page actions go under
the title, and the collapsed row has no breadcrumb. Each row is split 60/40
between the title or breadcrumb and the actions from 672px up, and 75/25 below.
Page actions given as descriptions fold as a set into a "Page actions" menu
button when they don't fit; a node of your own stays as it is. The action bar
gives its trailing items to an overflow menu. The title truncates on one line,
with its full text in a tooltip. Its controls sit one size down from the density
around it. `frame="flush"`, the default, is the quiet surface and its hairline;
`ghost` paints the ground it sits on, with no hairline. `corners="rounded"`
rounds it in the page or in a card.

## Motion

- **Collapse and expand:** one layout transaction. The header's height leads,
  and the breadcrumb and the tabs move from where they were to where they land,
  on its clock. The clock is the step for the farthest travel, one step faster
  collapsing, on standard-productive.
- **Title swap:** the title doesn't travel. Two copies swap in place, out then
  in. Collapsing, the large title rolls up a line and away through a soft edge,
  then the compact one rolls up into place from a line below, landing with the
  header. Expanding is the mirror. The breadcrumb's last slash comes and goes on
  the same beats.
- **Details:** the subtitle, `children` and the tags fade in place on the
  title's beats, out collapsing and in expanding. The edge that moves with the
  header cuts them where it ends.
- **The jump:** every button that changes place jumps: into or out of a menu
  button, between its rows and the collapsed row, and on a resize. Copies of the
  leaving buttons shrink out 4px, opaque, over the first third of the clock.
  Then the arriving ones grow in from 4px small over the rest. In a collapse or
  expand the clock is the move's; on a resize it is fast-01. A button within 4px
  of its old place stays put.
- **Ghost hairline:** with `frame="ghost"`, the collapsed row's hairline fades
  in on fast-00 as the compact title lands, and out as the large one starts
  back.
- **Soft edge:** content scrolling under the header leaves into a soft edge at
  its bottom, which grows over the first 16px of content under it.
- **The room it keeps:** collapsing on the scroll, it keeps the room it gives up
  as a margin below, moving with its height. Nothing below moves, the page's
  height doesn't change, and a short page collapses too. A collapse from
  `collapseHeader` or the toggle keeps no room: the content follows the header.
- **No fade:** with `motion={{ fade: false }}`, the titles roll through a hard
  edge, the edge over the details is hard, and the slash comes and goes on its
  beats with no fade.
- **Interruption:** a turn mid-move goes on from where everything is. An edge
  the scroll crosses mid-move is answered when the move lands. A resize across
  672px changes the layout at once.
- `timeScale` multiplies the clock.

## Reduced motion

It collapses and expands at once, and the buttons don't jump. On the scroll it
still keeps the room it gives up.
