# Pagination

The footer of a long list or a data table. On the start side are the page size
select and the item range. On the end side are the page select, the page count,
and back and forward buttons. It takes Carbon's props: `page` (from 1),
`pageSize`, `pageSizes`, `totalItems`, `pagesUnknown`, `isLastPage`, the text
props, `disabled`, `size` and `onChange`. The selects are the kit's inline
Dropdown, one size down from the bar. The buttons are the kit's ghost
IconButtons. A new page size goes back to page 1. Reaching the last page moves
focus to the back button; reaching the first moves it to the forward button. A
bar narrower than its controls scrolls sideways in the kit's Scroller.

## Motion

- **Content swap:** only what changes with the page swaps, out then in. The
  range's "11–20" swaps along x, in the page's direction. The page number swaps
  along y: forward, the old number leaves upward and the new one comes up from
  below; back, the other way. The old text moves 8px and fades on fast-01,
  exit-productive. Then the new one comes in from the other side on fast-02,
  entrance-productive. A change with the page unmoved, such as a new page size,
  comes from the end side. The first text is simply there.
- **Glide:** the rest of the range text, "of 103 items", is not swapped. It
  moves from its old place to its new one on the swap's clock, on
  standard-productive. In right to left it moves the other way.
- **Page count:** "of 11 pages" changes at once. It follows the page size, not
  the page.
- **Lists:** the page size and page lists are the Dropdown's, on the Menu's
  surface.
- **Rollover:** the selects and the buttons are full round. An icon-only
  button's hover is a circle.
- **Tooltips:** the back and forward buttons share one `TooltipGroup`.
- **Slow motion:** `timeScale` slows the lists, the swap and the scroller.

## Reduced motion

The swap is a snap: the new text is there on one frame, with no fade, and the
rest of the text is there at once. The lists and the rollover change at once.
Tooltips show and leave at once.
