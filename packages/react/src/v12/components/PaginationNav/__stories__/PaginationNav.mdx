# PaginationNav

Page numbers between a back and a forward button. The first and last pages
always show. Between them is a window of numbers, and the pages hidden on each
side sit behind a "…" that opens a list of them. It takes Carbon's props: `page`
(from 0), `totalItems` (the number of pages), `itemsShown` (default 10, at least
4), `loop`, `disableOverflow`, `size` (default lg), `tooltipAlignment`,
`tooltipPosition`, `translateWithId` and `onChange(index)`. `variant` sets the
marker's color: `inverse`, the default, or `hero`, the brand blue. `motion`
takes the selection kit's dials; keys left out take the kit's. `onMove` is
called as each glide of the marker starts.

## Motion

- **Selection marker:** the current page's mark is an underline at the row's
  foot, as line tabs draw it. It glides from page to page, timed by travel, its
  leading edge ahead. The page label takes its selected weight, 600, and color
  where the marker covers it.
- **Selection gesture:** a press on a page reaches the marker toward it. The
  release picks it. A press on the back or forward button reaches it toward the
  page that button steps to, and the click picks that page. Released off the
  button, the marker reaches back.
- **Window shift:** when the window of numbers shifts, the row reflows as one
  strip. The numbers that stay slide to their new places, timed by travel. The
  marker moves on the strip's clock, so a step that shifts the window by one
  leaves it still while the numbers pass under it.
- **List reflow, carry:** a number leaving slides on with the strip and fades
  out, faster. A number arriving slides in and fades up.
- **Soft edge:** the window's numbers sit between soft edges, 16px at most and
  never wider than the room to the next icon box. A number travelling out is
  gone before it reaches a "…". One arriving where a "…" went waits until it has
  gone.
- **Overflow list:** the "…" opens the kit's floating listbox of the hidden
  pages, the Dropdown's, on the Menu's surface. It snaps from the press and
  folds back into it. It shows five and a half rows and scrolls.
- **Rollover:** the numbers and the "…" take the kit's rollover, full round: a
  circle, or a pill on a long number. Hover adds a glow and the label steps
  brighter. The press insets 1px and stays round while the marker reaches toward
  it.
- **Tooltips:** the back and forward buttons are the kit's ghost IconButtons, in
  one `TooltipGroup`.
- **Keyboard:** the "…", closed: ↓, Enter or Space open the list on the first
  page, ↑ on the last; Home and End too. Open: ↑ and ↓ move, without wrapping;
  Home and End; Page Up and Page Down move 10; digits find a page; Enter or
  Space choose; Escape and Tab close. The current page has
  `aria-current="page"`. A polite live region says "Page n of N".

## Reduced motion

Nothing travels. The marker fades across to the new page, on a shorter clock.
The numbers land at once; leaving and arriving ones fade, on a shorter clock.
The overflow list is there or gone at once. The rollover changes at once.
Tooltips show and leave at once.
