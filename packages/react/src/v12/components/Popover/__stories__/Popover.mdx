# Popover

A surface opened from a trigger, for content that is not a list of actions:
filters, settings, a short form. `Popover` and `PopoverContent` take Carbon's
props. With `anchor`, the popover is a portalled panel opened from that element
instead. There is no caret: the surface sits 4px off its trigger. The content
has no padding of its own; the host pads it.

## Motion

- **Open:** the frame grows from its cause, as geometry: the press on the
  trigger, else the trigger's center. The content slides out of it on y and
  never scales. Opened from the start, with no gesture, it is there with no
  motion.
- **Close:** the far edge folds back toward the cause. The surface is gone on
  the fold's last frame.
- **Fade:** it fades up over the first part of the open and out over the last
  part of the close: 40ms on a clock of 220ms or less, else 80ms.
  `exitFade={false}` folds back opaque.
- **Interrupted:** reopened while it leaves, it turns round from where it is.
- **Soft edge:** on every side where the container clips the content,
  `min(16px, h, H − h)` per side, from the snap's live geometry. Only the
  content is masked, and there is no mask at rest.
- **Timing by size:** a large popover, such as a form, opens and closes on the
  slower surface clock.
- **Cast shadow:** off by default. `shadow` or `dropShadow` turns it on; it
  follows the surface on every frame.
- **While open:** scrolling and resizing place it again once a frame, with no
  motion.

## Reduced motion

There at once and gone at once: no growth, slide, fade or soft edge.
