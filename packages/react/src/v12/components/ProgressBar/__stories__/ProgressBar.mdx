# ProgressBar

An amount of work as a line, with Carbon's props: a label, the bar, helper text
and a status mark at the bar's end. With no `value` it is indeterminate. It adds
`variant` (neutral or hero), `pulse`, `mark` and `motion`.

## Motion

- **Fill:** travels from where it is to the new value on standard-productive,
  its clock from the distance. The fill's line grows 1px from 15% to 85% of the
  work, in the same tween.
- **Burst:** at most one new tween per `fast-02`. An update inside that window
  retargets the running tween from the fill's place and speed.
- **Loop:** with no value, a segment up to 40% of the track crosses it, one pass
  per loading period, on standard-productive. Every bar on a page is in one
  phase.
- **Loop and value:** a value picks up from the segment's live edges. A fill
  that loses its value leaves toward the end, a step faster, and the loop joins
  at the next pass.
- **Finished:** the fill travels to the end; once it lands, the bar takes the
  success colour and the check swaps in, then plays once.
- **Error:** the error colour at once, and the fill stays where it failed. With
  no value, the segment leaves toward the end.
- **Status colour:** crossfades on `fast-02`, linear.
- **Status mark:** swaps in place, out then in.
- **Pulse:** `pulse`, while active with a value: a highlight crosses the filled
  part to the leading edge, one pass every two loading periods. It never passes
  the leading edge.
- **Right to left:** the bar is mirrored, so the fill, the segment and the pulse
  run from the right.

## Reduced motion

The fill is at its value at once. With no value, three short still segments
stand in for the loop. No pulse. The status mark snaps in, with no beat, and the
status colour crossfades on half the clock.
