# ProgressIndicator

A task's steps on a line, drawn as line tabs. Its props are Carbon's
`ProgressIndicator` and `ProgressStep`, plus `reach` from `useProgressReach`.
Each step has an icon, a label and an optional secondary label. The current step
carries a bar on the line, and a fill runs over the complete steps.
`ProgressIndicatorSkeleton` draws it loading.

<Canvas of={ProgressIndicatorStories.Flow} />

## Motion

- **Bar:** the selection's bar glides to the current step by travel, its leading
  edge ahead by a stretch that scales with the track.
- **Reach:** a press that will move the bar, on a step or on Back and Next
  through `useProgressReach`, leans it toward that step. A press let go
  elsewhere reaches back.
- **Line:** one fill to the current step, on the bar's clock, so the two land
  together. Without a bar move, on its own travel's duration.
- **Icons:** each step's icon changes to its new state in place.
- **Rollover:** a step you can choose takes the press shrink.
- **Layout:** a resize or a change of direction places the bar and the line at
  once.

## Reduced motion

No travel and no reach: the bar fades across to the new step. The line and the
icons are there at once.
