# ProgressRing

An amount of work as a ring, for small places such as a file chip, a row or an
uploader item. It is the Spinner's sibling, with ProgressBar's prop names: the
loud arc over the Spinner's quiet base, a label and helper text. It comes in the
Spinner's sizes, 16, 20, 24 and 32px, neutral or hero. With no `value` it is the
Spinner.

## Motion

- **Draw-on:** with a value, as it mounts or takes another mark's place, the arc
  draws on from 12 o'clock, clockwise, as the track fades up. It runs on
  entrance-productive, its clock from the arc's travel, never under
  `moderate-01`.
- **Value:** the arc's end travels from where it is to the new value on
  standard-productive, its clock from the distance. The line grows with it, from
  its rest width at 15% to twice it at 85%, in the same tween. The outer edge
  holds, so the line grows inward.
- **Burst:** at most one new tween per `fast-02`. An update inside that window
  retargets the running tween from where the arc is.
- **Hand-off from turning:** a value that arrives while the Spinner turns is one
  move. The turn slows so the arc's tail lands at 12 o'clock while its length
  eases to the value. It leaves at the Spinner's own speed, so it never stops
  and restarts.
- **Finished:** the arc completes the ring. Then the status hand-off, out then
  in, with no fades: the ring shrinks 4px on `fast-01`, the success disc grows
  from 4px under its size on `fast-02`, and its check draws on, `fast-02`.
- **Error:** with a value, the arc turns red where it failed, finishes its
  travel there, then hands off to the error disc. With no value, the whole
  Spinner, its base too, turns red as it shrinks.
- **Back to active:** from a mark, the mark fades out on `fast-02`, then the
  ring or the Spinner draws on.
- **Value removed:** the ring closes, its tail catching its head, as the track
  fades, on `fast-02`. Then the Spinner draws on.
- **Right to left:** the arc runs counter-clockwise.

## Reduced motion

Values land at once, with no draw. The hand-off, the close and the marks are
there at once. With no value, the Spinner's three still dots stand in for the
turn.
