# RadioButton

A circle for choosing one option from a group. `RadioButton` and
`RadioButtonGroup` take Carbon's props, plus `timeScale`. The group holds the
choice, its legend and one message line; it is in a row by default. `RadioMark`
is the circle and dot alone, for a control that draws a radio it doesn't own.
`RadioButtonSkeleton` is the radio button while it loads.

## Motion

- **Dot in:** the fill changes at once. 40ms later the 6px dot grows from its
  center, half size to full, on fast-02 with entrance-productive. The state
  reads first.
- **Dot out:** the dot shrinks and fades on fast-01 with exit-productive.
- **Hover:** the rollover on the circle. Off, a wash of the text color at 8%;
  on, a toned step of the fill. The glow fades in on fast-02 and out on fast-01.
  The circle grows 1px. Hovering the label plays it too, however the label
  wraps.
- **Press:** no press color of its own. The circle presses 1px in.
- **Status ring:** `invalid` and `warn` color the circle's stroke and add 1px
  outside it, red or yellow. The rollover paints the ring, so it grows with the
  hover. A group's status rings every button in it. Disabled and read-only show
  no status.
- **Message line:** helper, warning and error share one line and swap in place,
  out then in.
- **Keyboard:** a native radio group. One tab stop; the arrow keys move the
  choice and wrap, and the dots follow. Read-only changes nothing.
- **Focus:** keyboard only, 2px `$text-primary`, 2px outside the circle; 3px out
  while a status shows. It has no transition.

## Reduced motion

The dot appears and leaves at once. The hover changes at once. The press still
shows; the release and the return to hover are at once. The message line changes
at once.
