# Recipes

Screens put together from the parts, each for one job. A recipe fills its
regions with the parts a team passes it and owns the rest: the frame, the
states and the motion between them.

## Assistant

An assistant beside the page: ask in words, see the reply's work as it
happens, and keep the page in view. `Assistant` is a `ChatPane` beside the
page holding a `ChatShell`: the header with the questions' navigator and
Close, the conversation, the prompt with its file chips and add menu, and
suggestions under the prompt until the conversation starts. Avoid it for a
single action with fixed inputs (use a form or a modal) or for help text (use
a toggletip).

The pane opens its own space, the page narrowing on every frame of it. Send
swaps to Stop; the reply's status row arrives, its steps joining under it,
then its words. Closed from inside the pane, focus goes back to where it was
when the pane opened, or to the page's launcher (`launcherButtonRef`).

## Resource index

Find, filter and act on many items of one kind. `ResourceIndex` puts the app
shell round the screen: `header`, `nav`, and in the workspace the
`pageHeader`, the `toolbar` row, the `filters` in use, `main`, the `batch` bar
while rows are selected, the `footer`, and the `panel` of filters. `state`
sets what the regions show: `loading`, `first-use`, `loaded`,
`filtered-empty`, `batch`, `panel-open` or `error`. Use it for roughly 10 or
more items that people search, filter and act on together; avoid it for a
handful (use a list) or for one item (use a details page).

<Canvas of={RecipesStories.ResourceIndexStates} />

A change of what main shows is a content swap, out then in. Loading to loaded
is the table's own reveal, and the footer arrives with the table's last row.
An empty state replaces the table whole, footer included.
`ResourceIndexScreen` is the screen without the shell, for a shell of your
own.

## Create flow

Create one item that needs a few groups of settings, in order, with a review
before it is made. `CreateFlow` is a tearsheet holding a step flow: the steps
in a column beside the form, the current step in the main area, and Cancel,
Back and Next (the submit words on the last step) in the footer. Each open
starts on the first step; the values are the caller's. Avoid it for a handful
of fields (use a modal).

## Reduced motion

Each recipe's parts keep their own reduced forms. The assistant's pane fades
in and out. A create flow's steps snap, and its tearsheet fades.
