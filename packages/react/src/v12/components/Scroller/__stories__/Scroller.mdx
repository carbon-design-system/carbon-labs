# Scroller

A floating pill per axis in place of the native scrollbar, over native scrolling
on a container or the window. The pill reads out where you are, and you can drag
it or press beside it. Wheel, trackpad, touch and keys scroll natively. Size the
root like any box; the viewport inside fills it and scrolls. `WindowScroller`
puts the pill on the window's edge and hides the page's own scrollbar while it
is mounted. The rest color is `$border-strong`, so it steps with the layer.

## Motion

- **Readout:** the pill's offset follows the scroll 1:1, never eased. Its length
  is the viewport's share of the content, at least 32px.
- **Length:** a change in the content's size glides the length over fast-02, on
  standard-productive. A change in the viewport's size follows at once.
- **Arrival:** scrolling, the pointer near the edge, or keyboard focus brings
  the pill in: a fade over fast-01, linear, while its thickness grows from 90%
  over fast-02, on entrance-productive. A run of activity never replays it.
- **Leave:** 1000ms after the last activity it fades over fast-02, on
  exit-productive. Never while it is hovered, pressed, dragged or focused.
- **Hover:** a 16px strip along the edge thickens the pill from 4 to 6px about
  its center line, fast-02 in and fast-01 out, with the hover color step.
- **Press:** the pill insets 0.5px on every side, with the pressed color step,
  held while dragging.
- **Squash:** dragged past either end, the far end comes toward the wall, up to
  4px, and springs back over fast-01 on release.
- **Track press:** a press beside the pill scrolls a page (87.5% of the
  viewport) toward it, or jumps to the press. The duration follows the pill's
  travel, 40 to 150ms, on standard-productive. A wheel, touch, key, drag or
  another press stops it where it is.
- **Soft edge:** a side of the viewport is masked only while content is past it,
  `min(16px, half the viewport, the amount past)`. None at rest at the top, and
  none where something is stuck to that side.
- **Corner:** with both axes, each track stops short of the corner by the other
  pill's hover footprint and the inset.

## Reduced motion

Fades only, on the reduced clock. The thickness does not grow, the length does
not glide and the squash does not spring back. A track press jumps.
