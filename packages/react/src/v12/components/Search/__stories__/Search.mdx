# Search

Carbon's Search on the text input's field: a magnifier at the start, the input,
and a clear button at the end. `labelText` is hidden and names the search
landmark. The clear button, or Escape with a value, empties the field, calls
`onChange` and `onClear`, and puts focus back in the input. It does not show
when disabled or read-only. `ExpandableSearch` is its magnifier button until
pressed, then grows into the field and takes focus. Emptied and left, or on
Escape in an empty field, it closes back into the button; `isExpanded` holds it
open. Its open width is `100%`, or `--cds-search-expanded-inline-size`.
`SearchSkeleton` is the search while it loads.

## Motion

- **Expand:** an expandable search grows from its button's box to its full
  width, and closes back into it. The clock follows the travel: under 24px
  fast-02, under 120px moderate-01, under 480px moderate-02. It opens on
  entrance-productive and closes one step faster, on exit-productive. The width
  moves as one layout change, so what sits beside it moves on the same clock. A
  turn mid-move takes the step of what is left.
- **Content with the opening:** shut, the content waits 8px back toward the
  button. Opening from shut, it starts at half the clock and comes into place
  over fast-02, on entrance-productive. It is laid out at its open width
  throughout, so its text never reflows. Closing, it holds still. Reopened while
  closing, it resumes from where it is.
- **Soft edge:** while it moves, the trailing edge fades over up to 16px, so
  nothing pops as it lands.
- **Fill and hairline:** opening, the field's fill and hairline fade in on
  fast-02. Closing, they fade out on fast-01 once it has closed.
- **Clear button in:** the first typed character brings it in as the snap's
  mark: from half size, opaque, growing about its center on fast-02, on
  entrance-productive. It keeps its place while hidden, so the text never
  reflows. Typing never moves it.
- **Clear button out:** emptied, it leaves one step faster, on fast-01 and
  exit-productive, back to half size and fading.
- **Hover:** the field is the magnifier's icon frame. Hovering anywhere on it
  plays the magnifier's hover. Not while disabled or read-only.
- **Rollover:** the magnifier button and the clear button take the press shrink,
  never the hover expand.
- **Outline ladder:** the text input's: hover, press and focus on the outline. A
  press on a focused field holds its focus edge.

The clocks are read from the token variables where the search is, so a page's
slow motion reaches them.

## Reduced motion

No travel and no scale. An expandable search opens or shuts at once, and its
content fades in on fast-02 at half the clock. The clear button only fades, on
the reduced clock. The fill, the outline and the magnifier's color change on
fast-01 at half the clock.
