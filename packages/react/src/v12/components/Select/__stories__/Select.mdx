# Select

Carbon's Select, a native `<select>`, on the kit's text field: a label, the
field with a chevron and a status mark at its trailing edge, and one message
line. `Select` takes Carbon's props, and its options are `SelectItem` and
`SelectItemGroup`. The select fills the field, so a press anywhere on it opens
the list. Where the browser lets the page style the list, it is drawn in the
page with the Dropdown's look; elsewhere it is the platform's own. Read-only
takes focus but never opens. `inline` puts the label beside a quiet field.
`SelectSkeleton` is the select while its value loads.

## Motion

- **Outline ladder:** the text input's. Hover moves the hairline a step, a press
  steps it further, and focus is the accent at 2px. The fill never changes.
- **Error border:** an error settles in once.
- **Message line:** helper text, warning and error share one line and swap in
  place, out then in.
- **Status mark:** a change is the content swap: the old mark leaves on fast-01,
  then the new one arrives on fast-02. From no status, it arrives at once.
  `statusBeat` plays the mark's icon once as it arrives. A choice is one act,
  not typing, so a status shows at once.
- **Icon hover:** the field is the chevron's icon frame, so the chevron's motion
  plays on the field's hover. It does not turn.
- **List snap in:** the page-drawn list grows from the field's center on its
  near edge, its width from 25% and its height from 75%, over fast-01 on the
  entrance curve.
- **List snap out:** the far edge pulls back to 70% over fast-01 on the
  pull-back curve, and the list is gone on its last frame.
- **List fade:** the list fades up over the entrance's first fast-00 and out
  over the exit's last, linear.

The platform's own list has no motion of ours.

## Reduced motion

The outline and the error border change on half of fast-01. The message line and
the status mark change at once. The page-drawn list fades in at its final size
on the reduced clock, and closes at once.
