# SelectList

Pick one value from a vertical list of rows. It is the content switcher's
vertical sibling, on the selection kit's track, selector and keyboard model.
Rows are `SelectItem`s, with an optional icon, secondary text and a trailing
accessory; `SelectItemGroup` adds headings and `SelectItemDivider` a line. With
no value, nothing is marked.

## Motion

- **Glide:** a pick sends the selector from where it is to the new row, on the
  content switcher's timing by travel. Its leading edge runs up to 24px ahead,
  then both edges land together. A new pick mid-glide starts from where the
  selector is.
- **Reach:** a press on another row moves the leading edge a few px toward it
  before the release. Slide to another row to retarget; slide off the list to
  cancel.
- **Drag:** the selector follows the pointer and lands on the nearest row, with
  a 4px squash at the ends.
- **Arrival:** the first pick grows the selector in from the row's center, 4px
  short, over fast-02. Clearing the value shrinks it back and fades it, over
  fast-01. A value already set on mount is placed at once.
- **Hover:** unselected rows take the rollover; a pressed row takes the pressed
  color while held. A motion icon plays on its row's hover.
- **Keyboard:** ↑, ↓, Home, End and typeahead move between rows, skipping
  disabled ones. With `activation="automatic"` they pick; with `manual` they
  move focus and Enter or Space picks.
- **Loading:** placeholder rows stand in the rows' own boxes. The rows take them
  in order as one reflow, and the track's height follows on the same clock.

## Reduced motion

No travel: the selector fades in on the new row as it fades out of the old, on
half the clock. There is no reach. The arrival and the clearing fade in place,
on half the clock. The pressed color still shows; the hover's release is at
once. A loading track changes height at once. `reduced` overrides the preference
for the selector and the hover.
