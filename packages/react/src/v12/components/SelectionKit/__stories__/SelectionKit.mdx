# SelectionKit

What the v12 selection components (content switcher, tabs, select list, menu)
share: the selection engine's hooks, the selector's faces and styles, the list
model (`useSelectionList`), and `SelectionPanels`, the content a selection
switches. Each component imports `selection-kit.scss`; the motion comes from the
motion package.

<Canvas of={SelectionKitStories.Panels} />

## useSelectionList

- **Roles:** one ARIA contract per role. A `tablist` of `tab`s, each joined to
  its panel by `aria-controls`; a `radiogroup` of `radio`s; a `listbox` of
  `option`s.
- **Tab stop:** one, on the selected item, or the first enabled item when there
  is none.
- **Keys:** the arrows along the list, Home and End, and typeahead when the list
  gives labels. With `automatic` activation a key selects; with `manual` it
  moves focus, and Enter or Space selects.
- **Right to left:** on a horizontal list, ← goes to the next item and → to the
  previous. A vertical list's keys do not change.

## SelectionPanels

- **Out:** the old content moves 8px the way the selector goes, and fades.
- **In:** once it has gone, the new content follows from 8px behind. Out then
  in, never overlapping.
- **Box:** `fit="content"` (the default) resizes the container to the new panel,
  starting with the in. `fit="largest"` holds the largest panel's size and never
  resizes.
- **Direction:** `travel="with"` follows the selector on screen, right to left
  included; `against` goes the other way.

## Reduced motion

A snap: the new panel replaces the old on one frame, with no travel or fade, and
the container resizes at once.
