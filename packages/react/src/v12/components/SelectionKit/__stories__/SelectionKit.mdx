# SelectionKit

What the v12 selection components (content switcher, tabs, select list, menu)
share: the selection engine's hooks, the selector's faces and styles, and
`SelectionPanels`, the content a selection switches. Each component imports
`selection-kit.scss`; the motion comes from the motion package.

<Canvas of={SelectionKitStories.Panels} />

## SelectionPanels

- **Out:** the old content moves 8px the way the selector goes, and fades.
- **In:** once it has gone, the new content follows from 8px behind. Out then
  in, never overlapping.
- **Box:** `fit="content"` (the default) resizes the container to the new panel,
  starting with the in. `fit="largest"` holds the largest panel's size and never
  resizes.
- **Direction:** `travel="with"` follows the selector on screen, right to left
  included; `against` goes the other way.

## Reduced motion

A snap: the new panel replaces the old on one frame, with no travel or fade, and
the container resizes at once.
