# Side nav

The app's page navigation, on the selection family's look and motion. It is a
`<nav>` of real links, not a tablist: each `SideNavLink` is an `<a href>`, or a
router link through `as`, and the current page is `aria-current="page"`.
`SideNavGroup` is a collapsible group of links under a header. A group opens
when you go to one of its pages and never shuts on its own. With `collapsed`,
`defaultCollapsed` or `collapsible`, and an icon on every top-level row, the nav
folds to a rail of icons. In an AppShell the shell draws the fold toggle;
elsewhere it sits at the nav's foot. `filter` pins a field at the top that
narrows the nav to the matching links. Every link and group header is a tab
stop.

## Motion

- **Glide:** within a group, the selector glides to the new page on the kit's
  timing by travel, its leading edge ahead. A press reaches toward the pressed
  link; slide to another link to retarget, or off to cancel. There is no drag.
- **Jump:** between groups, by default, the old selector shrinks 2px on every
  side, opaque, and is gone. Then the new one comes in at the new link from 2px
  in on every side, growing to full. The whole jump runs on fast-01: a third for
  the out, on exit-productive, and two thirds for the in, on entrance-snap. The
  hover on the new row shrinks with the old selector.
- **Landing:** a clicked row keeps its hover round the selector until the
  selector lands on it.
- **Pass:** with `crossGroup="glide"`, the selector glides between groups too.
  It is clear over each group header it passes, soft at the edges.
- **Group:** opening and shutting is the shared disclosure: the step for the
  travel, a shut one step faster. The links wait 8px above and come down over
  fast-02 once the body is half open. They hold still on the way out.
- **Group bar:** when the group holding the current page is shut, the selector
  leaves and a 2px bar marks the group's header. It comes in from half height,
  opaque, on entrance-productive over fast-02. It goes back to 70% on
  exit-productive over fast-01, fading as it ends.
- **Arrival:** when the selector has nowhere to come from, as on a row that an
  opening group reveals, it grows from 4px smaller, opaque, over fast-02 on
  entrance-snap.
- **Rail:** folding and unfolding is one motion. The width moves on
  standard-productive, its duration from the distance, a fold one step faster.
  The content holds still while the moving edge clips it under a soft edge.
  Unfolding, the labels slide in from the icon gap on a clock one step longer
  than the width's. Open groups fold on the same clock.
- **Overlay:** with `expandOnHover`, the rail expands over the page after the
  pointer rests on it for 250ms, and folds again when it leaves.
- **Ghost container:** with `frame="ghost"`, an open group is a container that
  grows out of its header's hover on the group's clock. Shutting, it shrinks
  back to the row, then fades over fast-01. With `shadow`, it casts the cast
  shadow, lit from the pointer. The shadow fades in as the container grows and
  out as it shrinks.
- **Still:** with `still`, or while there is a filter query, groups open and
  shut at once, and the selector arrives and leaves at once. A move to a new
  page still glides.
- **First paint:** no motion. The groups that hold the current page mount open.

## Reduced motion

Within a group, the selector fades in at the new page while it fades out at the
old one, at half the duration. A jump puts the selector at the new page at once.
A group opens or shuts at once, and its links fade in on the reduced clock. The
bar and the ghost container change at once. The rail's width changes at once and
its labels only fade: out before a fold, in after an unfold.
