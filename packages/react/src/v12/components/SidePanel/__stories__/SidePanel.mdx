# Side panel

A panel at the side of the page, for a task beside it. It has a top bar with the
close button, a title block, an optional toolbar, a scrolling body and up to
three footer actions. It is controlled: every way of closing calls
`onRequestClose`, and only `open` closes it. `frame` sets what it stands on:
flush against the edge, inset 8px from the edges, or floating over the page.
With `includeOverlay` the page dims and focus stays in the panel. With `slideIn`
the page makes room instead of being covered.

## Motion

- **Snap by its edge:** a flush or inset panel grows from the middle of the edge
  it is attached to. Its width starts at nothing and its height at 60%. Under
  4px wide it fades with its width, so no sliver shows.
- **Axial timing:** by its edge, the width takes the whole clock, set by the
  larger axis's travel. An md panel takes moderate-03, 300ms. The height is done
  at 0.55 of that, 165ms.
- **Snap, floating:** a floating panel grows from the press that opened it, or
  from its launcher's center when a key opened it, clamped into its box. Its
  height starts at 75% and its width at 25%. Over 320px it takes the larger
  surface's timing.
- **Content arrival:** from the first frame, the content moves 8px into place
  from where the panel grows: along x by its edge, along y floating. It runs on
  entrance-productive, one step or 60ms longer than the longest axis clock,
  whichever is sooner. A 300ms width gives 360ms.
- **Collapse exit:** leaving, the far edge pulls back to the near one, the other
  axis held and the content still. By its edge, that is the edge it is attached
  to. Floating, it pulls back toward the edge nearest its cause, to 70%, fading
  over its last 40ms or 80ms by its clock, and is gone on its last frame.
  `exit="reversed"` plays the entrance back instead.
- **Push:** with `slideIn`, the page's margin on that side moves on the panel's
  width clock and curve, in and out, so the gap holds. The room is the panel's
  width plus an inset panel's distance from the edge. A floating panel never
  pushes.
- **Overlay:** with `includeOverlay`, the dim fades in over moderate-01
  (entrance-productive) and out over fast-02 (exit-productive), from wherever it
  is.
- **Step swap:** a new `currentStep` swaps the body out, then in. Past the first
  step a back button shows.
- **Condensed title:** with `animateTitle`, the title scrolls with the body.
  Once it is under the top bar, the bar takes its fill and divider, and the
  small title fades in and rises 4px over fast-02.
- **Tooltip hold:** the tooltips of its icon buttons wait until the panel and
  its content have landed, then open against their final box.
- **Turning round:** reopened while it leaves, the content goes on from where it
  is.

## Reduced motion

By its edge, the panel only fades, at its final geometry, on half the clock. A
floating panel is there or gone at once. The content has no lead. The page makes
room at once. The overlay fades on half the clock. A new step shows at once. The
condensed title fades without rising.
