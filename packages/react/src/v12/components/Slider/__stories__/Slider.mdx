# Slider

A value or a range on a line. `Slider` takes Carbon's API: one knob, or two with
`unstable_valueUpper`, the number fields beside it, `invalid` and `warn` with
one message line under it. It adds `shape` (line, track or knob), `steps` for a
few named stops, `snap` and `ticks`, `fillStyle`, `knobShape`, `status`,
`gradient`, `reference`, `ai` and `loading`. `SliderSkeleton` is the slider
while it loads, at its own size.

## Motion

- **Glide:** a key or a click sends the knob from where it is to the new value,
  on standard-productive. Its duration comes from the distance. The leading edge
  runs a little ahead, then both land together. A held key's repeats move at
  once.
- **Press:** a press on the line starts the knob toward the pressed point. The
  release lands there.
- **Drag:** the knob follows the pointer exactly and squashes against the ends
  and the other knob. A drag begun from a press glides to the pointer first.
- **Snap:** with `snap`, the knob glides stop to stop as the pointer passes each
  halfway point. With `sticky`, the default, it holds until the pointer is a
  quarter stop past halfway, at most 8px, and leans up to 3px toward the pull.
- **Hover and press:** hover grows the knob 2px. A pointer press takes back 1px.
  A press with no hover, a key or a touch, insets it 1px. On fast-01.
- **Fill:** rides the knob's live center on every frame. With `fillStyle` both,
  hover steps it toward the knob's color and from 2px to 3px; a press keeps the
  hover's weight. `contrast` is the color only, `thickness` the weight only. On
  fast-02, standard-productive.
- **Stop dots:** on the track shape, the dot under the pointer grows, and a
  press on it glides the knob there. While dragging, the stop the knob left
  shows as a short pill.
- **Status:** a change of `status` wipes the new color in from the knob along
  the fill, with a soft edge, on a glide's clock for the fill's length. On a
  range the two wipes meet midway. A change mid-wipe stops the old one where it
  is and wipes in over it.
- **Message:** the old message fades out on fast-01, then the new one fades in
  where it is. An error fades in on moderate-02.
- **Meeting:** two pills swell into one shape as they near. Two circles fill
  their overlap. On one value they join as one pill with a line at the seam; the
  seam grows on moderate-01, entrance-productive, and goes on fast-01,
  exit-productive. Let go within 4px of each other, they snap together. Two
  triangles stop where their stems meet, and the shared stem grows 2px taller.
- **Readout:** with `steps` and `readout`, the step's label rolls as it changes,
  up and down or, with `readoutDirection="horizontal"`, across.
- **AI:** with `ai` on the track, dots cover the fill and the knob is their
  lens. A change sends a wave from the knob's new place. On the special step the
  dots come in from the knob, each on slow-01, entrance-productive; leaving,
  they go from the track's start to the knob, each on moderate-03,
  exit-productive, over a slow-01 sweep. There the knob becomes a disc in the AI
  color, scaling in on moderate-02, entrance-expressive, and out on fast-02.

## Reduced motion

No glide: the knob moves to the new value and fades in there, on half the clock.
A snap is the same fade. There is no press reach and no lean. A status lands at
once. The message changes at once. The readout swaps in place. The joined seam
and the triangles' stem change at once. The AI dots come and go at once and hold
still, and the AI disc scales on fast-01. The knob's hover and press keep their
clock.
