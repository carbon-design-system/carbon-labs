# SplitButton

A primary action, and beside it a chevron that opens a menu of related actions,
with Carbon's ComboButton props. Not for two equal actions: use two Buttons. One
shell in two cells, on the Button's look: `kind` primary or secondary, `variant`
filled, outlined or tonal, sizes xs to lg. `busy` shows a spinner in the
action's place while it runs.

## Motion

- **Hover:** the Button's glow in each cell, clipped to the cell, with no
  growth.
- **Press and release:** the Button's pressed color and release circle, clipped
  to the cell, with no press-in.
- **Chevron:** it turns while the menu shows.
- **Menu:** the Menu's open and close, from the press point or, with
  `openFrom="trigger"`, the chevron's center. The menu is at least as wide as
  the shell.
- **Busy:** out, then in. The label fades out, then the spinner draws on; the
  reverse as busy ends.
- **Focus:** keyboard only. Each cell has its own ring, round outside and square
  at the seam.

## Reduced motion

The glow and the press show at once, and the release circle lands at once. The
chevron changes at once. The menu is the Menu's: there at once and gone at once.
Busy swaps at once.
