# Step flow

A create flow in steps: a progress indicator, one step's heading and fields at a
time, and Back and Next, with Submit on the last step. `StepFlow` draws it all
in place. `useStepFlow` returns the flow, so its parts can go anywhere:
`StepFlowProgress` for the indicator, `StepFlowBody` for the steps, and
`StepFlowActions` for the buttons, or `stepFlowActions(flow)` for a tearsheet's
`actions`. Back always moves. Next and Submit move only past valid steps; a
step's `invalid` holds Next on it, and its `disableSubmit` disables Next or
Submit. `onCancel` adds Cancel before Back. Each step is a region named by its
heading. Steps stay mounted while hidden, so Back keeps what was entered. Once
the old step has gone, focus moves to the new heading and the step is announced,
"Step 2 of 3: Access" by default.

## Motion

- **Content movement in place:** a step change is the content swap along x, out
  then in, the way the flow goes. The old step leaves 8px, fading, on fast-01
  (exit-productive). Then the new one comes 8px from the end side on fast-02
  (entrance-productive). Back runs the other way, and right to left mirrors it.
- **Height:** the body's height holds through the out, then glides to the new
  step's height with the in, standard-productive.
- **The marker's glide and reach:** the progress indicator's own. Back and Next
  reach the marker toward their step as they are pressed.
- **An invalid step:** Next pressed on it moves nothing. The step shows its
  errors, its indicator step turns invalid, and focus goes to its first field in
  error.
- `timeScale` slows the swap and the buttons down.

## Reduced motion

A step change is a snap: the new step is there on the frame the old one goes,
with no travel or fade, and the height changes at once. The indicator takes its
own reduced motion.
