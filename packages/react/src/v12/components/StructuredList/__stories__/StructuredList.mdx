# StructuredList

Rows of related details in columns, read across. `StructuredListWrapper` holds a
`StructuredListHead` and a `StructuredListBody` of `StructuredListRow`s and
`StructuredListCell`s, with the roles of a table. Columns size to their content,
with a hairline between rows. With `selection`, each row is a choice: a radio
mark in its leading cell, and a marker on the selected row. `isCondensed` gives
8px cells; `isFlush` removes the start padding, but not with `selection`.

## Motion

- **Static:** without `selection`, the list has no hover and no motion.
- **Hover:** with `selection`, a body row takes the rollover: the quiet card
  hover, as an even wash. Rows press flat. The cells' text steps up to primary
  on the hover's clock. The selected row takes no hover and no release circle.
- **Selection glide:** the marker travels from the row it is on to the new one,
  timed by travel (40, 80, 120 or 160ms), its leading edge ahead. The first pick
  grows it in from its row's center. A row selected at first is placed at once.
- **Reach:** a press on another row reaches the marker toward it. Sliding to
  another row retargets; sliding off the list cancels. There is no drag.
- **Line frame:** with `frame="line"`, the default, the marker is the two row
  lines it lands on, 2px, with no fill and no sides. It is drawn the whole time
  it shows, so nothing switches on or off as it travels.
- **Ghost frame:** with `frame="ghost"`, the marker is a wash over the row.
  While it reaches or stretches across rows, the lines inside it hide and it
  carries a line on each edge.
- **Radio mark:** the dot grows from its center when chosen, over fast-02, and
  shrinks and fades when unchosen, over fast-01.
- **Keyboard:** each row's `StructuredListInput` is a hidden radio. They form
  one group with one tab stop; the arrow keys move and select. The row takes a
  2px ring for the keyboard only.
- **Slow motion:** `timeScale` slows the marker and the hover.

## Reduced motion

The marker does not travel: it fades in at the new row while a ghost fades out
at the old, on half the clock. The hover changes at once. The radio's dot
changes at once.
