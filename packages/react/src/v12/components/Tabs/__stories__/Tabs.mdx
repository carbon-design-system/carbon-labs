# Tabs

Move between views of one context. `Tabs` holds the state for a `TabList` and
its `TabPanels`, with Carbon's API: `selectedIndex`, `defaultSelectedIndex`,
`onChange` and disabled tabs. Line tabs, the default, ride a 2px underline on a
hairline; `frame="inset"` puts them in a track with a selector. A `Tab` takes an
icon before its label and a second line. `IconTab` is an icon only, named by its
tooltip, with an optional dot for something new. With `dismissable`, tabs close.
`TabsVertical` and `TabListVertical` are the vertical form.

## Motion

- **Glide:** the selector, the underline or the inset selector, travels from
  where it is to the new tab, on standard-productive. Its duration comes from
  the distance, from fast-00 to moderate-01. Its leading edge stretches ahead. A
  new selection mid-glide starts from where the selector is.
- **Labels:** a label takes its selected face where the selector covers it:
  primary text, and heavier, held to its regular width. In line tabs it changes
  under the underline's span. On vertical tabs' `line` frame only the move's two
  ends change, and the bar slides past the tabs between.
- **Press:** a press on another tab reaches the selector toward it before the
  release. Slide to another tab to retarget; slide off to cancel. The pressed
  tab takes the pressed color while held.
- **Drag:** on by default. The selector follows the pointer and lands on the
  nearest enabled tab, with a 4px squash at the ends.
- **Landing:** a tab just picked keeps its hover round the selector until the
  selector has landed.
- **Hover:** unselected tabs take the rollover and a label step to primary. A
  motion icon plays on its tab's hover.
- **Tooltip:** an icon tab's name, and a label cut short, show in a tooltip on
  hover or keyboard focus. The pointer can move onto it.
- **Panels:** out, then in, along the list's axis, the way the selector moved.
  The old content moves 8px and fades, on fast-01 and exit-productive. The new
  content follows from 8px behind, on fast-02 and entrance-productive.
- **Panel box:** with `TabPanels fit="content"`, the default, the container
  resizes to the new panel as a window. `fit="largest"` holds the largest
  panel's size. With vertical tabs' `height`, the list and each panel scroll
  instead.
- **Close:** a dismissable tab closes with its × or Delete, which calls
  `onTabCloseRequest` with its index; remove the tab in response. The closed
  tab's copy fades as its space closes and the tabs after it slide in, on one
  clock and the glide's curve. The clock is the glide's for the farthest travel.
  A closed selected tab's selector glides on into the tab that takes the
  selection. Focus moves at once, to the tab that took its place, or the one
  before it if it was the last.
- **Overflow:** a list too long for its width scrolls, with no scrollbar. A
  scroll button fades in at each end that has more, on fast-02, and out on
  fast-01, with a 16px soft edge inside it. The selected or focused tab is kept
  clear of both. The buttons are out of the tab order.
- **Keyboard:** ← and → wrap and skip disabled tabs, ↑ and ↓ on vertical tabs;
  Home and End go to the ends. With `activation="automatic"` they select; with
  `manual` they move focus, and Enter or Space selects. In right to left, ← goes
  to the next tab and → to the previous.

## Reduced motion

No travel: the selector fades in on the new tab as it fades out of the old, on
half the clock. There is no reach. The pressed color still shows; the hover's
release is at once. The panels snap: the new one replaces the old on one frame,
and the container resizes at once. A closed tab is gone at once, with no copy.
The list scrolls to a tab, or by a page, without smooth scrolling. Tooltips show
and leave at once, with no drift.
