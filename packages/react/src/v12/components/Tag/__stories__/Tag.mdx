# Tag

A short label: a status, a kind, a count. `Tag` is Carbon's read-only tag. It is
content, not a control, and never answers the pointer. `SelectableTag` is a tag
the person turns on and off, on the rollover. `DismissibleTag` is a tag the
person can remove, with a close at its end. `TagSkeleton` is a tag while it
loads. They take Carbon's props, plus `corners`, `arrive`, `show` and
`timeScale` where they apply.

## Motion

- **Snap from below, in:** a tag that appears in UI already on screen snaps in
  from below, 2px smaller, 3px at lg, up to full from its own center. It runs on
  moderate-01 with entrance-productive, and fades up over its first 40ms.
  `arrive="auto"` (the default) snaps it only when it arrives on its own, not
  with the page, card or list it came in; `true` always; `false` never.
- **Snap from below, out:** `show={false}` snaps it out, one step faster, on
  fast-02 with exit-productive, down to the same scale. It fades out over its
  last 40ms, then it is gone. Unmounted, it goes at once.
- **Stillness:** only the first tag of a run moves. The run is warm while a snap
  plays and for 400ms after the last one ends; a tag that appears then is simply
  there. Tags that appear on the same frame snap together.
- **Turn:** shown again mid-exit, or hidden mid-arrival, it turns round from
  where it is, on the share of the clock its travel leaves.
- **Rollover:** a SelectableTag's hover is a glow under the cursor, a flat press
  and a release from the point of release. Its fill changes on the rollover's
  clocks and it never moves. The DismissibleTag's close takes the rollover too,
  as a circle on its glyph.
- **Close icon:** hovering anywhere on a DismissibleTag plays its close icon.
  Enter, Space, Delete or Backspace on the close dismisses it. Focus moves to
  the next dismissible tag, or the previous one after the last. `onClose` asks
  the host to remove the tag.

## Reduced motion

Tags arrive and leave at once, with no snap. A rollover's press still shows; its
release and re-hover are at once.
