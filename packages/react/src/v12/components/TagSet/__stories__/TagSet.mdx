# TagSet

A row of tags that fits its container. As many tags show as fit; the rest go
into a "+N" that opens them in a popover. With more than
`allTagsModalSearchThreshold` (10) in the overflow, the popover shows that many
and a "View all tags" button, which opens a modal of every tag with a search.
Each tag is a `label` and the kit Tag's props; one with `onClose` is a
DismissibleTag. `maxVisible` caps the row, and `multiline` wraps it instead.
`overflowType="tag"` lists the overflow as tags, `default` as text.
`TagSetSkeleton` is the set while it loads.

## Motion

- **Reflow, out then in:** when the width moves tags into or out of the
  overflow, the tags leaving snap out where they are, as the Tag's out. Then the
  "+N" moves. Then the tags coming back snap in, staggered, as the Tag's in.
- **"+N" slide:** the "+N" slides from its old place to its new one, its clock
  from its travel over the tags between the two counts.
- **"+N" count:** the number rolls on the slide's clock. On a dismissal it
  waits, then rolls as the gap closes.
- **"+N" arriving or leaving:** when nothing overflowed before, it snaps in
  after the tags have left. When nothing overflows now, it snaps out first,
  before the tags come back.
- **Dismissal:** a tag taken out of `tags` snaps out where it was. Several leave
  20ms apart. Once the last has started, the gap closes in one move, its clock
  from its travel.
- **Arrival:** a tag added, or back from the overflow, snaps in once the gap has
  closed. The tags already there hold still.
- **Popover:** the kit's anchored Popover. It grows from the press point and
  leaves by folding back into it. It flips to stay on screen.
- **Modal:** the kit's Modal. Its search filters the tags out, then one reflow,
  then in, staggered.
- **Bursts:** changes less than 200ms apart land at once.

The set's tags never snap in on their own: the set plays their snap, once, on
its clock. The first fit is the set as it is, with no motion.

## Reduced motion

No slide, roll or snap: the tags and the "+N" change at once. A dismissed tag is
gone at once, and a tag added is simply there. The popover is there at once and
gone at once. The modal takes its own reduced motion.
