# Tearsheet

A large sheet that rises from the bottom of the page for a task that needs room.
It has a header with a label, a title, a description and optional header
actions, a navigation row, an influencer column beside a scrolling main area,
and up to four footer actions. It follows `open`, and closes itself unless
`onClose` returns `false`. A sheet with no actions is passive: it has a close
button, and a press outside closes it. Escape closes the topmost sheet. Focus
moves in on open, Tab wraps inside, and focus goes back to `launcherButtonRef`,
else to what had it, as the sheet starts to leave. The page doesn't scroll while
a sheet is open. `TearsheetNarrow` is centred and has no influencer, navigation
or header actions. With `warm`, on by default, the sheet mounts hidden, with its
content, while its launcher is hovered or focused.

## Motion

- **Snap rise:** the sheet enters by its bottom edge, from the middle of it. Its
  height starts at nothing and its width at 60%. Joined to the bottom, it takes
  the sliver guard: it is opaque from 4px tall, so no sliver shows.
- **Axial timing:** the height, the axis it enters along, takes the whole clock,
  and the width leads, done first. The clock comes from the sheet's size on the
  travel steps, one step shorter.
- **Collapse exit:** leaving, the top edge comes back down to the bottom at full
  width, two steps shorter than the entrance. The content holds still and the
  clip passes over it. The bottom stays square on every frame.
- **Content arrival:** the content rides up with the sheet along its height,
  from the first frame, over a short travel. It lands just after the sheet's
  longest axis clock.
- **Dim and blur:** the overlay and one even blur over the page come in on the
  sheet's clock, entrance-productive, and go out on its exit clock,
  exit-productive.
- **Page recede:** the page behind draws back on the sheet's clock,
  standard-productive, and comes back on the exit clock. `backdrop` leaves the
  scale or the blur out, or both with `false`.
- **Stack depth:** a sheet opened over another makes the one below recede to 98%
  per level about its top and lift 16px per level, so its top shows above the
  new one. It dims and rounds its bottom corners, on the arriving sheet's clock,
  standard-productive. It comes forward again on the leaving sheet's clock. A
  stack has one dim and one page recede, owned by its bottom open sheet.
- **Tooltip hold:** the tooltips inside wait until the sheet and its content
  have landed.
- `timeScale` slows the motion down.

## Reduced motion

The sheet only fades, at its final geometry, on the reduced clock. Its content
doesn't move. The dim and the blur fade on the reduced clock, and the page
doesn't scale. In a stack, the sheet below recedes, lifts and rounds its corners
at once, and its dim fades on the reduced clock. Tooltips don't wait.
