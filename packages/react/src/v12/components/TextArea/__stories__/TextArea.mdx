# TextArea

Carbon's TextArea on the text input's field: the same label row and counter,
fill, outline, status mark and message line. It is `rows` lines tall, 4 by
default, and one row is the size's field height. It resizes vertically only;
with `cols`, disabled or read-only it does not resize. The status mark sits at
the top of the trailing edge, on the first line, and its room is kept, so a
status never re-wraps the text. `enableCounter` with `maxCount` shows
`count/maxCount` in the label row; `counterMode` counts characters or words.
Read-only and disabled beat both statuses, and invalid beats warn.
`TextAreaSkeleton` is the text area while it loads.

## Motion

- **Outline ladder:** the text input's. The outline carries hover, press and
  focus. A press on a focused field holds its focus edge.
- **Error border:** an error's 2px border settles in once, on moderate-02. It
  never shakes.
- **Message line:** helper text, warning and error share one line and swap in
  place, out then in. The old text fades out on fast-01, then the new one fades
  in where it is. An error fades in on moderate-02, with the border. Nothing
  slides.
- **Status mark:** a change is the content swap, 8px down: the old mark leaves
  on fast-01, then the new one arrives on fast-02. From no status, it arrives at
  once. `statusBeat` plays the mark's icon once as it arrives.
- **Late status:** an error or warning that rises while typing waits for a pause
  (`typingPauseMs`, default 1000ms) or blur. It leaves on the keystroke that
  fixes it. `lateErrors={false}` shows every status at once.
- **Scroller:** text past the rows gets the kit's pill in place of the native
  bar, and a soft edge the text leaves into as it scrolls.
- **AI fill:** a filled value arrives by the content swap, 8px up, clipped to
  the field. A revert plays it back down.

## Reduced motion

The message line, the status mark and an AI fill change at once. The outline and
the error border change on half of fast-01. A rising status still waits for the
pause or blur. The pill does not grow, glide or squash; it only fades.
