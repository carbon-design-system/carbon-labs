# TextInput

A single-line field: a label, the field with its outline and a status mark at
its trailing edge, and one message line for helper text, a warning or an error.
`TextInput` takes Carbon's props. `PasswordInput` adds a toggle that shows and
hides the password. `MultiFieldInput` is one field in segments, each with its
own value and validation. `TextInputSkeleton` is the field while its value
loads. `FieldMessage` and `FieldStatusMark` are the message line and the mark,
shared with other fields.

## Motion

- **Outline ladder:** the outline carries every state. Hover moves it halfway
  from `$border-subtle` to `$border-strong`, in on fast-02. A press steps it to
  `$border-strong` at once, still 1px. Focus is the accent at 2px, on fast-01.
  Leaving any state takes fast-01. Disabled and loading fields have no ladder.
- **Placeholder:** while the field is empty, the placeholder rises toward
  `$text-secondary` with the ladder: a step on hover, more pressed and focused.
- **Error border:** an error settles in once, 1px to 2px of `$support-error`, on
  moderate-02 with entrance-productive. It never shakes. While focused, the
  focus edge wins.
- **Message line:** helper text, warning and error share one line and swap in
  place, out then in. The old text fades out on fast-01, then the new one fades
  in where it is: helper or warning on moderate-01, an error on moderate-02,
  with the border. Nothing slides.
- **Status mark:** a warning or error mark at the trailing edge. A change is the
  content swap, 8px on y: the old mark leaves on fast-01, then the new one
  arrives on fast-02. From no status, it arrives at once. `statusBeat` plays the
  mark's icon once as it arrives.
- **Late status:** an error or warning that rises while typing waits for a pause
  (`typingPauseMs`, default 1000ms) or blur. One that falls shows on the
  keystroke. `lateErrors={false}` shows every status at once.
- **Loading:** the value is a bar at the expected width; the label, frame and
  controls are drawn. When it loads, the bar dissolves in place as the value
  fades up.
- **AI fill:** a filled value arrives by the content swap, 8px, coming up from
  below, clipped to the field. A revert plays it back down.
- **Stillness:** nothing moves while typing.

## Reduced motion

The message line, the status mark and an AI fill change at once. The outline and
the error border change on half of fast-01. A rising status still waits for the
pause or blur.
