# Theme zone

An area of the page in a theme of its own, through Carbon's `Theme`: `light` or
`dark`, `page` (no zone of its own) or `inverse` (the opposite of the theme
round it). On a page in a v12 theme, `light` and `dark` are v12 light and v12
dark (the zone classes `cds--v12-light` and `cds--v12-dark`), and `inverse`
inside v12 light is v12 dark, and the reverse. Elsewhere they are white and g100
(`cds--white`, `cds--g100`).
