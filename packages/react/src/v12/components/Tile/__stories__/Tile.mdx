# Tile

A surface for content, with 8px corners and 16px in, on `--cds-surface`. `Tile`
is plain and has no hover. `ClickableTile` is a link with `href`, else a button.
`SelectableTile` holds a checkbox as its tab stop. `TileGroup` and `RadioTile`
choose one of a set. `ExpandableTile` shows `TileAboveTheFoldContent` and opens
to show `TileBelowTheFoldContent`. `TileSkeleton` is a tile while it loads. They
take Carbon's props, plus `hoverSteps`, `timeScale`, `footer`, `toggleArea` and
`onExpandedChange`. `decorator`, `slug`, `light`, `hasRoundedCorners`,
`tileMaxHeight` and `tilePadding` are accepted and do nothing.

## Motion

- **Hover, text tile:** the rollover is painted behind the content, never over
  it. It steps off the fill: quiet by default, half the house step on light
  themes and three quarters on dark, so it reads as one step in both, or
  standard with `hoverSteps`. It fades in on fast-02 and out on fast-01, and the
  surface grows 1px. A press shows the pressed color at once and insets the
  surface 1px.
- **Hover, tile with controls:** a tile holding a checkbox, a radio or another
  control hovers with a 1px `$border-strong-01` inset ring and keeps its fill.
  It has no press and no growth. Pressed, its hairline takes `$text-secondary`
  at once.
- **Controls inside:** the innermost control owns the hover and the press. Over
  one, the tile goes to rest on fast-01, and fades back in on fast-02 when the
  pointer is on the tile again. The tile's own checkbox, radio and toggle keep
  the tile's hover. A click on a control inside never toggles, chooses or
  follows the tile.
- **Corner icon:** the clickable tile's motion arrow plays on hover and keyboard
  focus. Motion icons in its content play with it.
- **Selection:** a steady 2px ring in the selection color, with no transition.
  The hover and press tint under it. The checkbox and the radio mark take their
  own motion.
- **Fold:** the shared disclosure. The fold opens on the step for its travel,
  entrance-productive, and shuts one step faster, exit-productive. It is
  clipped, never scaled, under a soft edge of `min(16px, h, H − h)`. It is one
  layout change on the tile's container, so what is below is pushed once, and
  tiles there that change together move as one motion. A toggle mid-move turns
  from where the fold is.
- **Fold content:** opening, it comes 8px down into place from the first frame
  and settles just after the fold lands. Shutting, it holds still. On a turn it
  resumes at once.
- **Expandable tile hover:** one quiet hover step, with no press or release over
  a surface that grows. The toggle has its own text rollover in its letters. Its
  chevron points down when shut and up when open.
- **Above the fold:** with `toggleArea="above-the-fold"`, the wash goes to rest
  on fast-01 over the opened part, and comes back over the top part and the
  toggle's row.
- **First paint:** no motion.

## Reduced motion

The hover and the ring change at once. The press still shows; the release and
the return to hover are at once. The icons hold still and change state at once.
The checkbox and radio marks appear at once. The fold opens or shuts at once,
with no soft edge, and its content fades in on the reduced clock.
