# TimePicker

Carbon's TimePicker on the kit's text field: a short field for the time
(`hh:mm`) and its selects beside it, under one label, over one message line.
`TimePicker` takes Carbon's props, including `warning` and `warningText`. Its
children are `TimePickerSelect`s, native `<select>`s for AM / PM or a time zone.
They take the picker's size, its `disabled` unless they set their own, and its
`readOnly`, which holds their value.

## Motion

- **Outline ladder:** the text input's, on the field and on each select. Hover
  moves the hairline halfway to `$border-strong` on fast-02, a press steps it to
  `$border-strong` at once, and focus is the accent at 2px. Leaving takes
  fast-01.
- **Error border:** the text input's: an error settles in once.
- **Message line:** warning and error share one line and swap in place, out then
  in.
- **Status mark:** the text input's. A change is the content swap: the old mark
  leaves, then the new one arrives. A status shows at once, with no wait for a
  pause in typing.
- **Field width:** the field widens for the status mark at once as a status
  shows. When it goes, the field keeps its width for fast-01, then narrows.
- **Icon hover:** each select is its chevron's icon frame, so the chevron's
  motion plays on the select's hover.

The selects' lists are the platform's own, with no motion of ours.

## Reduced motion

The outline and the error border change on half of fast-01. The message line and
the status mark change at once.
