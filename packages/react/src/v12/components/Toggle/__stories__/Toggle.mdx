# Toggle

A switch for a setting. It is a track with two stops, and its knob is the
selection family's selector. `Toggle` takes Carbon's props: `labelText`,
`labelA` and `labelB` for the state text, `size`, `toggled`, `defaultToggled`,
`onToggle`, `disabled` and `readOnly`. It adds `corners`, `variant`, `mark`,
`disabledReason`, `motion`, `timeScale` and `onMove`. It is a `role="switch"`
button, and Space and Enter flip it. `ToggleSkeleton` is the toggle while it
loads.

## Motion

- **Glide:** a click, a key or a change of `toggled` moves the knob as the
  family's selector moves. Its leading edge runs ahead, at most half the travel.
  The duration comes from the distance.
- **Color:** the on color is a fill that rides the knob's live place. The knob's
  color follows its place too, so the two ease together over the glide, or with
  the pointer in a drag.
- **Mark:** the status dot sits at the stop the knob isn't on. It fades on
  fast-02. A hero or status track never shows it.
- **Press:** anywhere on the track, the knob included, the knob reaches 2px
  toward the other side at once. At `md` the track also shrinks 1px a side; at
  `sm` it doesn't. A grabbed knob insets 1px.
- **Drag:** the knob follows the pointer and stops at the walls with the kit's
  squash. It lands on the nearer side.
- **State text:** both texts share one cell, so the row keeps its width and
  nothing beside it moves.
- **Tonal track:** with `variant="tonal"`, the track takes its hue from where it
  sits.
- **Hover:** none. The reach on a press and the inset on a grab are the
  feedback.
- **Focus:** keyboard only, 2px `$text-primary`, outside the track with a 2px
  gap. It has no transition.

## Reduced motion

The knob doesn't glide. It fades in on the side it lands on and out on the
other, at half the duration. The color changes at once.
