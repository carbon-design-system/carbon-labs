# Toggletip

A button that opens a small high-contrast panel on click, for help that holds a
link or an action, where a tooltip can't. The parts take Carbon's props:
`Toggletip`, `ToggletipLabel`, `ToggletipButton`, `ToggletipContent` and
`ToggletipActions`. It is built on the kit's Popover and takes its props. A
click on the button toggles it. Escape closes it and returns focus to the
button; a press outside, focus leaving or the window losing focus closes it. The
panel is rendered in place, so Tab goes from the button into it.

## Motion

- **Open:** the Popover's floating snap. The frame grows from the press on the
  button, or from its center by keyboard, and the content rides it on y. Opened
  from the start, with no gesture, it is there with no motion.
- **Close:** the far edge folds back toward the button. The panel is gone on the
  fold's last frame.
- **Fade:** it fades up over the first part of the open and out over the last
  part of the close: 40ms on a clock of 220ms or less, else 80ms.
  `exitFade={false}` folds back opaque.
- **Interrupted:** pressed again while it leaves, it turns round from where it
  is.
- **Soft edge:** on every side where the frame clips the content,
  `min(16px, h, H − h)` per side.
- **Button:** the rollover in a 26px full-round fill: a neutral glow, grown 1px
  on hover, a press back to rest, then the release. While open, the selected
  fill.
- **Icon:** the Information motion icon plays on hover.

## Reduced motion

The panel is there at once and gone at once: no growth, slide, fade or soft
edge. The button's press still shows; its release and re-hover take the 1ms
floor.
