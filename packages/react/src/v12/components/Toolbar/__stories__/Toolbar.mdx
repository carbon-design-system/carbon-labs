# Toolbar

A row or a column of controls with one tab stop. `ToolbarButton` is an action
and `ToolbarToggle` a toggle: a ghost icon button whose label is its tooltip and
its name, or a labelled button. `ToolbarToggleGroup` holds toggles that belong
together: `single` is a selection track with one choice, `multiple` keeps each
toggle's own pressed fill. `ToolbarGroup` keeps controls together, and
`ToolbarDivider` draws a hairline between them. In a row, what does not fit
moves into a More menu. The arrow keys move between controls, with Home and End;
they do not wrap unless `wrap` is set. In a single group, the arrows move focus
without choosing, and Enter or Space choose.

## Motion

- **Rollover:** every control takes the rollover's matched-lightness hover and
  press. There is no hover growth, and a press insets 1px. A motion icon plays
  on its control's hover.
- **Selection glide:** a single group's selector glides to the new choice on a
  clock set by its travel, as the content switcher's does. It reaches toward a
  press before the release, and it can be dragged.
- **Pressed fill:** a toggle's pressed fill is the variant's selector fill. It
  fades in on fast-02 and out on fast-01, on standard-productive.
- **Tooltip:** icon controls share one tooltip. It shows 100ms after the pointer
  arrives. Moving to a neighbor puts it there at once, its text changing in
  place. It leaves fast-02 after the pointer goes.
- **Snap mark:** when controls first move into the menu, the More button arrives
  as the snap's mark: from half size about its center, on fast-02,
  entrance-productive. It leaves at once. A toolbar that is already narrow when
  it mounts simply has one.
- **Overflow:** trailing controls leave the row and come back with no motion. A
  group moves as one, and no divider is left before the More button. Inside a
  layout transaction, the cut is made once, when the layout settles. In the
  menu, buttons are items, toggles are checkable items, a single group is radio
  items and a multiple group is a group of checkable items.
- **Tonal:** a tonal toolbar takes its hue from where it sits.

## Reduced motion

The selector does not glide, and there is no reach. The More button is there at
once. Tooltips show and leave at once. The pressed fill still fades.
