# Tooltip

Short labels and descriptions on hover and keyboard focus. `Tooltip`,
`IconButton`, `DefinitionTooltip` and `TooltipGroup` take Carbon's props and
share one look: the inverse fill, 4px corners and no caret. Escape dismisses a
tooltip, and the pointer can move onto it without it leaving. Only one tooltip
shows on the page at a time.

## Motion

- **In:** the first tooltip of a run waits its enter delay (100ms), then grows
  from the pointer, where it is when the tooltip snaps: width from 25%, height
  from 75%, on fast-01 and entrance-snap. The text slides on y and never scales.
- **From the keyboard:** it grows from the trigger's center, clamped into the
  box, and does not drift.
- **Fade:** it fades up as it grows and out as it folds, on the fade standard.
  `exitFade={false}` folds back opaque.
- **Warm:** while any tooltip shows, or within 400ms of the last one leaving,
  the next one is there at once at its final geometry: no delay, no snap, no
  fade. It leaves the same way, after its leave delay.
- **Moving on:** in a `TooltipGroup`, the one tooltip jumps to the next trigger,
  its text changing in place.
- **Drift:** while hovered, it follows the pointer along the trigger's edge, on
  one axis, at most 2px and 10% of its own size, on fast-02 and
  entrance-productive.
- **Out:** after its leave delay, the last tooltip of a run folds its far edge
  back into the trigger and is gone on its last frame.
- **Leave delay:** 120ms (fast-02) for a label, as on `IconButton` and
  `TooltipGroup`; 300ms (moderate-03) for text worth reading, as on `Tooltip`
  and `DefinitionTooltip`, so the pointer can cross onto it.
- **Interrupted:** back on the trigger while it leaves, it turns round from
  where it is.
- **Stillness:** a trigger that arrives under a resting pointer opens its
  tooltip only once the pointer moves more than 0.5px.

## Reduced motion

Every tooltip, the first one too, is there at once after its enter delay and
gone at once after its leave delay. There is no drift.
