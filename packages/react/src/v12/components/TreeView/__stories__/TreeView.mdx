# TreeView

A hierarchy of nodes to open, shut and select. `TreeView` holds `TreeNode`s; a
node with child nodes is a parent, and its caret opens and shuts it. One node is
selected, under the selection kit's selector. With `multiselect`, every row has
a checkbox and there is no selector. `variant` sets the selector's style and
`size` the row height. `TreeViewSkeleton` is the tree while it loads.

## Motion

- **Glide:** between two leaves of one group, the selector glides. All four
  edges share one clock from the longest travel, the leading edge ahead. Labels
  take the selected face where it covers them.
- **Jump:** every other move jumps: to another parent or depth, and to or from a
  parent's row. The old selector shrinks 4px and is gone, then the new one grows
  in from 4px small, on fast-01, a third out and two thirds in. The pointer, the
  keyboard and a change to `selected` or `active` take the same rule.
- **Landing hover:** the new row keeps its hover around the selector until the
  selector lands. On a jump, that hover shrinks with the old selector.
- **Press:** the selector reaches toward the pressed row. Slide to another row
  to retarget, or off to cancel. There is no drag.
- **Arrival and fold:** shutting a parent over the selected node takes the
  selector with the row, at once. Opening it again, the selector arrives on the
  row once the children have landed.
- **Disclosure:** a parent's children open and shut on the disclosure module, on
  the travel's step; shutting is one step faster. The children come down 8px
  with the opening, and the soft edge closes over them on the way out. Every
  node that changes in one commit is one motion on the tree.
- **Caret:** the caret turns on the same clock and curve as the children's
  opening.
- **Rollover:** every row takes the kit's rollover. The caret and icon step up
  on hover, in on fast-02 and out on fast-01. A motion icon plays on its row's
  hover.
- **Checkboxes:** with `multiselect`, a parent's box follows its enabled
  children: all checked, checked; some, mixed; none, unchecked. Toggling a
  parent sets every enabled node under it. Disabled nodes aren't counted.
- **Keyboard:** ↑ and ↓ move between visible nodes, with Home and End. → opens a
  parent or goes to its first child; ← shuts it or goes to the parent. Enter and
  Space select, and Enter also toggles a parent. Type-ahead finds a node. With
  `multiselect`, Space toggles a node, Enter selects it alone, Ctrl + A selects
  every visible node, and Ctrl + Shift + Home or End selects to the end. The
  tree is one tab stop.
- **Double-click:** on a parent's row, it opens or shuts the parent. Not on the
  caret or the checkbox.

## Reduced motion

No travel. A parent opens or shuts at once, its children fading in. A glide
becomes a fade: the selector fades in at the new node while a ghost fades at the
old. A jump puts it on the new row at once. The caret turns at once.
