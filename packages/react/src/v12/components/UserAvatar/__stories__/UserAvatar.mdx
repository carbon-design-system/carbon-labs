# UserAvatar

A person as their initials, their picture or an icon in a coloured circle, with
the props of Carbon for IBM Products' `UserAvatar`. The initials are the first
and last name's first letters, or a single name's first two; with no name it
shows the User icon, or `renderIcon`. Sizes are sm 24, md 32 (the default), lg
40 and xl 48px. The twelve colours follow the Carbon theme it sits in; `theme`
names one outright. `UserAvatarSkeleton` is its loading state.

## Motion

- **At rest:** nothing moves on its own: no hover, no press. It isn't a control.
- **Image:** the initials or the icon show until the image loads, then swap to
  it, out then in, never overlapping. The initials leave up 8px, fading, over
  `fast-01`; then the image comes up from 8px below over `fast-02`, clipped by
  the circle. `timeScale` slows the swap.
- **Already loaded:** an image loaded when the avatar first paints is simply
  there, with no swap.
- **New image:** the initials come back until it loads, then it swaps in. A
  broken image keeps the initials.
- **Tooltip:** with `tooltipText` the avatar is a button and the kit's Tooltip
  names it. It snaps in from the pointer. Along a run of avatars only the first
  tooltip snaps; the next ones appear in place.

## Reduced motion

The swap is a snap: the image is there on the frame the initials go. The tooltip
is there at once.
